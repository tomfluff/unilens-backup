# Session 2: numbers in the text, a Sources button, a longer voice pause, go-to, a participant panel

## Context

Session 1 of the co-design study (P01–P06) used the initial prototype. Two things came up for session 2:

- The 1.2 s pause that ends a voice message cut P03 off mid-sentence.
- Yotam wants the source numbers back inside the answer's text by default. Participants should be able to try the other ways: the words each source supports underlined, or the numbers after the answer. Sessions are remote, so the facilitator cannot press keys on the participant's computer.
- Participants asked UniLens to take them somewhere in their own words (P02: "take me to the notise"; P03: "Take me to the place on this page showing me the punctuality notifications for the months during 2026."). The matcher only takes short fixed phrases ("take me to the second one"), so these went to the model, which only described where to look.
- Participants should be able to change some settings themselves in session 2, but not all 90.

## Decision

- **`voicePauseMs`**, a setting (600–4000 ms), replaces the fixed 1.2 s. It is how long a pause ends a voice message recorded for the server's recognizer. The default is **2000 ms**. Pressing the microphone again still ends the recording at once.
- **The Sources button (`sourceStyleButton`)**: a button in the chat's header that cycles where the source numbers go:
  - in the text (the default);
  - in the text, with the words they support underlined;
  - after the answer.
  - Each press is announced ("Sources now: …") and logged. It sets the existing `citePlacement` and `associateText`.
  - While the button is shown, every answer asks for the supported words, so a switch to underlines is exact at once, for earlier answers too.
- **`?sources=inline|underline|end`** in the page address sets the starting style, so the facilitator can put it in a participant's link. `tunnel.sh link P03 2 underline` writes it.
- **Go-to (`assistantGoTo`)**: when the user asks to be taken to something, shown where it is, or to see it on the page, the model adds a go-to marker, `[[goto:nX]]`, with the inventory element's id. Once the answer is complete the widget acts on it the way pressing that source's number does: the page scrolls there if it is off-screen, and the element is outlined. There is no guard: the model decides (Yotam, 2026-10-09).
  - The marker shows as that element's citation, a single number even when the model also cites the element. It is never read aloud, and the history keeps it as a citation, so a later answer cannot copy it.
  - Each go-to is logged (`goto`) with the question that led to it, the element, its label, whether the page moved, or why nothing happened (the element is not a source of the answer, or no longer on the page). The export counts these apart from the sources the participant chose.
  - A late answer moves nothing: if the user closed the chat, or asked or Alt+clicked again before the answer finished, the go-to is logged as not done, with the reason.
  - The matcher still takes the exact short phrases at once, without the model.
- **A participant settings panel**: a preset may list the settings a participant may change (`panel`). In it, the gear (bottom left, "UniLens settings") opens a panel with only those settings, under the usual tabs; its search finds only them, and saving and loading settings files are hidden. The facilitator key Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S still opens every setting. For session 2 (agreed with the session lead): highlight colour, outline and backdrop; page zoom and the zoom keys; the page map; speak your question, read answers aloud, the reading voice; sounds; chat text size; high contrast. The source style stays on the Sources button, and the voice pause is not in it.
- **Only the default ways in session 2 (Yotam, 2026-10-12)**: participants ask by Alt+click, typing or the microphone, and are answered by highlights, the chat and reading aloud; every alternative is only discussed in the gallery, even when it is built. So the `session2` preset turns drawing a box (Alt+drag) off, keeps Live and the off-screen (edge) arrows off as in session 1 and takes both out of the panel, and starts with the page map off ("Minimap": Off / While zoomed / Always, still in the panel, for the walkthrough with page zoom).
- **Kept per participant (`keepPerParticipant`)**: in session 2 each task link, pasted in the Zoom chat, opens a new tab. So the session2 preset keeps a participant's changes (the panel, the Sources button) in the browser's localStorage under the preset and their pid, not in the tab. Tabs still open take up each other's changes. The facilitator's reset (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R) forgets them. Without a pid, changes last for the tab, as in session 1.
- **The highlight colour adapts to the page (`hlColorMode`, #26, Yotam 2026-10-12)**: a participant found a highlight that showed on one section's background vanish on another. "Adaptive color" (Look and feel, right after Highlight color; also in the session-2 panel) has four choices, each drawn on a light and a dark section in its tile. The band, brackets, underline, fill and glow follow it. The black and white ring and the thin border stay as they are, as do the numbers, the arrows and the page map.
  - **Off** (`fixed`): the highlight color as before. The initial prototype keeps it.
  - **Invert** (`invert`): the coloured parts are drawn in white, in a layer of their own under the numbers, blended with `mix-blend-mode: difference`, so they invert whatever is under them. The band inverts fully, so text it crosses stays readable. The fill and glow invert at most a quarter (a partial inversion greys text; at a quarter, black text on white keeps over 5:1).
  - **Best contrast** (`contrast`): from a palette of eight vivid colours (never black or white), the one whose worst contrast over the backgrounds under the highlight is highest. Near ties go to the earlier colour: yellow first, blue before red.
  - **Enhanced color** (`near`; "Near this color" until 2026-10-12, now second after Off): the chosen colour, its OKLCH lightness moved only as far as 3:1 over every background needs, within "How far the colour may change" (`hlColorRange`): a little (0.15), some (0.3), or as far as needed.
  - The backgrounds are read where the highlight is drawn: eight points just outside the element and five inside, each the stack of page elements under it, blended top-down. Gradients count each stop; same-origin images are sampled; anything unreadable counts as both black and white. A highlight across two backgrounds is judged by the worse. UniLens's own layers, chat and panels are never read. A colour whose fill or glow would push the element's text below 4.5:1 is left out.
  - Each box is re-read when it is drawn, at most every 200 ms while the page scrolls or changes, once more after it stops, and when an image loads.
  - The default is Off, in session 2 too (Yotam, 2026-10-12); the other three are in the participant panel. The UI spells it "color" (US spelling throughout).
- **"Both" source style (`sourceStyle: "both"`, #27, P04's request)**: a fourth style beside numbers in the text, underlined words and numbers after the answer. The numbers stay where the model put them, and every number appears again, once and in order, on a line after the answer. It sets `citePlacement: "both"` ("In the sentence, and again after the answer"); words are not underlined in it. The Sources button cycles in text → underlined → after → both, with its own icon, and `?sources=both` works in a link. The style setting now has tiles: a short two-source answer drawn by the chat's renderer, one per style. The session-2 default stays "In the text".
- **Read-aloud says the source numbers (`readSources`, #28)**: "Say the source numbers when reading aloud" (Conversation, Speaking and listening; also in the session-2 panel), off by default. With it on, reading an answer aloud (the Listen button, the folded header's, autoplay) says each number where the chat shows it, numbered as its chips: "(source 2)", "(sources 1 and 2)", and for the row after the answer "Sources: 1 and 2." Japanese: 「（根拠2）」, 「根拠：1と2。」. "Both" says them once, in the text. A failed answer says none. Not yet heard with either voice (no OpenAI credits); to check by ear before session 2.
- **A pulse (`hlPulse`, P02's request)**: "Pulse" in the highlight's settings and the session-2 panel, off by default. A ring in the highlight color, between black rims so it reads on any page, grows out from each highlight and fades, as the highlight appears: "A few times, then still" (three pulses) or "For as long as it shows". A source pressed again pulses again. Never under reduced motion. In forced colors it is drawn in the system's text color.
- **Lasting click marks (`clickMarks`, P02's and P03's request)**: "Keep a numbered mark where I clicked" (Conversation, and the session-2 panel), off by default. "What to mark" (Yotam, 2026-10-12): the spot clicked; what was clicked, outlined in the highlight's look as pressing its P entry in the chat outlines it, but lasting; or both (the default). Something too big to outline (a whole section), or gone from the page, is marked by its spot. "How the spot is marked" (`clickMarkStyle`, Yotam, 2026-10-12, from the options page): a map pin with its number in it; a dot with a line to its number (the default); the same with the dot see-through so the text under it shows; a soft glow with a line pointing at the spot; or only that line. The lines stop short of the spot where there is no dot, and every line is two-tone, a dark core with a white edge, so it shows on any background. The number turns below or to the left at the window's edges. Its number, P1, P2, … as the chat's place entries, wears the source numbers' look and size ("Number on the page"; the smallest when those are hidden), with the P that tells it from them. The marks are drawn over the highlights' outlines and under their numbers, follow what was clicked when the page moves or re-wraps (the spot is measured at the click, before the capture) and the clicked point when that is gone, and go when the conversation starts over. They sit outside the page, so captures never include them, and they never take a click. The outline keeps the chosen color: an adaptive one would sample the page on every scroll.
- **A floating button (`floatingButton`, #29; P04 asked for one, P03 forgot the assistant)**: "Floating button" in General and the session-2 panel, off by default (Yotam, 2026-10-12, from the options page). "Round button": a blue circle with the chat mark directly above the settings gear at the bottom left, the gear's size (Yotam: the two share a size), so it grows with the page's zoom as the gear does; "Remind me now and then" (`fabReminder`, shown for the round button only) puts "Ask me about this page" beside it for 8 s after 120 s without a question (`fabReminderSec`), never while the chat is open. "Bar with a microphone": "Ask about this page" and a microphone at the bottom center, as tall as the gear at 100% and that size on screen at any browser zoom. Both follow the interface size and high contrast, and step up above anything the page fixes at the bottom (a back-to-top button, a cookie banner), checked again as the page scrolls or changes. Pressing either opens the chat beside it about what is on screen, as an Alt+click in the middle of the window would (the backend is told so, as for a click); the microphone opens it listening; an open chat is brought to its field or its microphone instead. Such a place makes no click mark. The study log records `floating_button` and `fab_reminder`. The session-2 panel offers the button but not its reminder (Yotam, 2026-10-14: "It's more fit for session 3, agency and AI behavior"), so the reminder stays off for all of session 2.
- **A new answer shows from its start, with Listen first (`answerFromStart`, #30; P01 and P06, Yotam 2026-10-12)**: when an answer begins, the chat log scrolls once so the question just above it is at the top (or the answer itself, when the question would take more than 40% of the log, as at 400%), and then stays: it does not follow the text as it streams and does not jump when it ends, and if the user scrolls meanwhile it stays where they put it. A spacer after the last answer gives the question room to reach the top and shrinks as the answer fills it, and the browser's scroll anchoring is off in the log, so nothing moves the reader. Listen sits on its own row at the start of the answer, there (dimmed) from the first word, so it never re-wraps the text, also when auto-read turns it into Pause and Stop. The sources, the stepper, All and Back stay at the end. A preset value, not a participant's setting: on in `session2`, off in `initial`, which keeps session 1's behavior (the log follows the text, a long answer jumps back to its start when it ends, Listen at the end). Decision record: `.local/studies/01-guidance-codesign/answer-scroll-and-listen-2026-10-12.md`.
- **Zooming in one movement, and which moves glide (Yotam's try-out, 2026-10-12)**: Ctrl+scroll and Ctrl+plus zoomed in two stages on the study sites: the page grew, then slid. The sites set `html { scroll-behavior: smooth }` (kept, as many real sites do), and UniLens' own scroll that keeps the point under the pointer in place was a plain `scrollTo`, which the browser turned into a smooth slide. Every scroll UniLens makes is now instant, whatever the page's CSS (measured on the home and status pages: the point under the pointer drifted about 180 px per step before, under 1 px now). Yotam's rule for motion: moves to highlights glide (a source pressed, the stepper, a highlight or cue clicked) and so do the assistant's own (go-to); every move the user aims themselves is instant: zooming, wheel and key panning, and a click or drag on the minimap (which glided for about a second before).
- **The chat steps aside as the page moves, and is off to begin with (Yotam's try-out, 2026-10-12)**: "Move the chat out of the way of sources" stays in the session-2 panel but is off in `session2` (it was on). When it is on, the chat now moves at the moment the page starts its glide to a source, judged from where the source will land, instead of after the page has arrived (measured: about 1.1 s later before, at once now). An assistant's zoom on its way still waits for the zoom.

The click marks' P numbers never wait at the edge either.

The floating button's place is never marked.

## Consequences

- The initial prototype is unchanged, except that its voice pause is now also 2 s (the new default). The frozen session-1 build keeps its own copy until it is refrozen.
- Asking for the supported words adds a little to each answer in session 2.
- Go-to also answers "where are the notes" or "show me the title" by moving the page, because the model reads those as asking to be shown. Pressing the round arrow takes the page back.
- The chat never moves (as in session 1), so the place the page goes to can sit under the chat; the outline and its number show beside it.
- **Every cited place drawn, also inside a cited section (`hlFoldOuter`, Yotam 2026-10-14, for P02)**: the AI often cites a section together with several places inside it. Session 1 folded each such pair into the section's outline ("1·2·3"), so the places only showed when stepped through one at a time. Session 2 now draws the section's outline and each inner place's own outline and number. A pair still folds into the inner one when the outer adds nothing (the same text, or the inner covers at least 90% of it: the double outline of bug 3, 2026-09-26), and a link or button keeps its own outline as before. The initial preset keeps session 1's folding (`hlFoldOuter: true`); the setting is in Highlight's "more" group, not in the participant panel. Corner numbers that would sit on each other (a heading at its section's corner) take the first free spot to the left, then the right, then the rows below, always on screen (session 2 only; the initial preset places numbers as before).
