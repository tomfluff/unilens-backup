Weekly meal plan

Week of 5 October 2026

Seven family dinners for the week of the Lantern Festival, planned so that one cooking session feeds the next: extra chicken on Wednesday becomes Thursday's lunchboxes, double mushrooms on Thursday go into Friday's rice, and Saturday's market trip buys Sunday's fish. Prices are what I paid at the Harukawa Central supermarket for a family of four (two adults, two children).
