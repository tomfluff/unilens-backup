# Daily Table — candidate study tasks

Fifteen questions or goals a participant might bring while choosing a dish or cooking from the page. Each has the English and Japanese wording, where the answer is, why it is interesting for an assistant, and the answer. Pages are under `/en/` and `/ja/` with the same paths.

### 1. Cups instead of grams
- EN: "I only have measuring cups. How much bread flour is the 300 g in the milk bread, and how many grams is a cup of all-purpose flour?"
- JA: 「計量カップしかありません。食パンの強力粉300gはUSカップで何杯ですか？薄力粉1カップは何グラム？」
- Where: `recipes/milk-bread.html` (Metric/US switch on the card); `guides/measurement-conversions.html` (table, and a bar chart image with `alt="chart"`).
- Why: needs the unit switch or a second page; the guide warns that a Japanese cup is 200 ml and a rice cup 180 ml, not 240 ml.
- Answer: 2⅓ US cups of bread flour (130 g a cup). One US cup of all-purpose (or cake) flour is 125 g, spooned and levelled.

### 2. Sticky dough
- EN: "My milk bread dough is sticking to everything. Should I add more flour?"
- JA: 「食パンの生地が手や台にくっついて扱えません。粉を足したほうがいいですか？」
- Where: `recipes/milk-bread.html`, the story ("The dough will be sticky"), steps 3–4 and the notes.
- Why: the advice is spread between the long introduction and the steps; a participant mid-kneading wants it fast.
- Answer: It is normal with tangzhong. Add no more than 1 tbsp of flour; if it is unmanageable, cover it and leave it 10 minutes, then knead by slapping and folding with a scraper. After the butter goes in, keep slapping and folding 8–10 minutes until smooth and glossy (windowpane test).

### 3. Has it risen enough?
- EN: "How do I know the dough has finished its first rise?"
- JA: 「一次発酵が終わったかどうかは、どうやって見分けますか？」
- Where: milk bread steps 5–6, with the before/after step photos (no alt text).
- Why: the cue is visual and partly only in photos; a timer is on the step.
- Answer: About 50–60 minutes at 28–30°C, until doubled. Finger test: push a floured finger 2 cm into the centre; if the hole stays, it is ready; if it springs back, wait 10 more minutes; if the dough collapses it is over-proofed, so shape it straight away.

### 4. No mirin
- EN: "I don't have mirin for the nikujaga. What can I use, and is there an alcohol-free option?"
- JA: 「肉じゃがのみりんがありません。代わりに何を使えますか？アルコールなしの方法は？」
- Where: substitutions table on `recipes/nikujaga.html`; `guides/pantry-staples.html`.
- Why: answer in a table inside the card; needs applying to the recipe's amount (2 tbsp).
- Answer: For each 1 tbsp mirin, 1 tbsp sake + 1 tsp sugar, so 2 tbsp sake + 2 tsp sugar here. Alcohol-free: 1 tbsp water + 1 tsp sugar per tbsp (2 tbsp water + 2 tsp sugar). The pantry guide adds that "mirin-style seasoning" has under 1% alcohol.

### 5. The double fry
- EN: "For the karaage, what temperature and how long is each fry? I don't have a thermometer."
- JA: 「唐揚げの1度目と2度目、それぞれ何度で何分揚げますか？温度計がありません。」
- Where: `recipes/chicken-karaage.html` steps 3–6 (timers), notes; `guides/oven-temperatures.html` (oil table).
- Why: three numbers across several steps, plus a no-thermometer test in the notes and in another page's table.
- Answer: First fry at 160°C for 3 minutes, rest 4 minutes on a rack, second fry at 180°C for 1 minute; the centre should reach 75°C. Without a thermometer, dip a dry wooden chopstick: small bubbles rising slowly at 160°C, lots of bubbles at once at 180°C.

### 6. Scaling for five
- EN: "I'm making oyakodon for five. How many eggs and how much chicken do I need?"
- JA: 「親子丼を5人分作ります。卵と鶏肉はどれくらい必要ですか？」
- Where: `recipes/oyakodon.html`, servings scaler (base: 2 servings).
- Why: needs the scaler; the result has awkward halves (7½ eggs), and the steps keep the 2-serving wording ("half the broth" per bowl).
- Answer: ×2.5: 2½ chicken thighs (about 625 g), 7½ eggs (use 7 or 8), 375 ml dashi, 5 tbsp each soy sauce and mirin, 2½ tsp sugar, 1¼ onions, 1 kg cooked rice. Cook each bowl separately in the small pan.

### 7. Quick and vegetarian
- EN: "Which vegetarian recipes can I make in 30 minutes or less?"
- JA: 「30分以内で作れるベジタリアンのレシピはどれですか？」
- Where: `search.html` with the "Up to 30 min" and "Vegetarian" filters; or category and recipe cards.
- Why: needs filters or comparing times across many cards.
- Answer: Two: Agedashi tofu (25 min, vegan with kombu dashi) and Butter-soy mushroom pasta (20 min).

### 8. Pudding the day before
- EN: "Can I make the kabocha pudding the day before a party? How long does it need in the fridge?"
- JA: 「かぼちゃプリンはパーティーの前日に作れますか？冷蔵庫で何時間冷やせばいいですか？」
- Where: `recipes/kabocha-pudding.html` card times (Chilling 4 hrs), step 10, make-ahead and storage.
- Why: spread across the times row, a step and two card sections.
- Answer: Yes, up to 2 days ahead, covered in the ramekins; turn out just before serving. Chill at least 4 hours, ideally overnight. Keeps 3 days; does not freeze well.

### 9. Water for new rice
- EN: "How much water do I need for 2 gō of this year's new rice in a pot?"
- JA: 「新米2合を鍋で炊くとき、水は何ml？」
- Where: `guides/rice-water-ratios.html` (bar chart with empty alt, and the table).
- Why: the chart is an image without alt text; the table holds the same number; needs doubling.
- Answer: 180 ml per gō, so 360 ml (ordinary white rice is 200 ml per gō, 400 ml). Soak 30 minutes; boil 5–7 minutes, lowest heat 12–13 minutes, rest 10 minutes.

### 10. What's in season
- EN: "Is it still green yuzu season? When are yellow yuzu at their best?"
- JA: 「青ゆずの季節はまだ続きますか？黄ゆずがいちばんおいしいのはいつ？」
- Where: `guides/seasonal-produce.html`, the calendar image (`alt="calendar"`); partly in "What to buy in October".
- Why: the full answer is only in a chart image.
- Answer: Green yuzu: August–October, peak September–October, so this is the last of it. Yellow yuzu: November–January, peak November–December. Other peaks now (October): kabocha, sweet potatoes, chestnuts, log-grown shiitake, maitake and shimeji, persimmons, taro, new-crop rice, Pacific saury.

### 11. Salt for yuzu kosho
- EN: "I ended up with 60 g of zest and chillies for the yuzu kosho. How much salt do I use, and how long does it keep?"
- JA: 「ゆずこしょうの皮と唐辛子が合わせて60gになりました。塩は何g？どのくらい保存できますか？」
- Where: `recipes/yuzu-kosho.html` ingredients (15–20% note), notes, storage.
- Why: needs a calculation from a percentage; safety note in the notes.
- Answer: 15–20% of the weight: 9–12 g (never below 15%, which is 9 g). Mature one week in the fridge; keeps 6 months in the fridge, a year frozen.

### 12. The week's plan
- EN: "In this week's meal plan, which day takes the longest, which costs the most, and what does the week cost in total?"
- JA: 「今週の献立表で、調理時間がいちばん長いのは何曜日？いちばん高いのは？1週間の合計はいくら？」
- Where: `meal-plan.html` (wide table, footer row, inline chart).
- Why: comparing a wide table that scrolls sideways at high zoom.
- Answer: Longest: Saturday 10 Oct, 1 hr 10 min (lantern dango, karaage and tamagoyaki for the festival). Most expensive: Sunday 11 Oct, ¥2,480 (minato-nabe). Week total ¥11,220 and 4 hours of active cooking, not counting rice.

### 13. When the kombu comes out
- EN: "Making dashi: when exactly do I take the kombu out, and when do the bonito flakes go in?"
- JA: 「だしをとるとき、昆布はいつ取り出して、かつお節はいつ入れますか？」
- Where: `guides/dashi.html` steps and the temperature timeline image (empty alt).
- Why: a sequence with temperatures; the chart restates it visually without text.
- Answer: Soak 10 g kombu in 1 L water 30 minutes; heat on low-medium about 10 minutes and take the kombu out at about 90°C, when tiny bubbles cling to it, before it boils. Bring to the boil, add 20 g katsuobushi, turn off the heat, wait 2 minutes, strain without squeezing (about 900 ml).

### 14. Frozen patties
- EN: "Can I freeze the hamburg patties raw, and cook them straight from the freezer?"
- JA: 「ハンバーグは生のまま冷凍できますか？凍ったまま焼けますか？」
- Where: `recipes/hamburg-steak.html`, the first comment and the author's reply (also make-ahead).
- Why: the cooking-from-frozen method is only in a comment reply.
- Answer: Yes. Sear them frozen over medium-low heat 4 minutes per side, add 100 ml water, cover and steam on low about 15 minutes, to 75°C in the centre.

### 15. Sake lees and children
- EN: "The minato-nabe has sake lees. Is it OK for my children, and what can I use instead?"
- JA: 「みなと鍋には酒かすが入っていますが、子どもが食べても大丈夫？代わりに何を使えばいいですか？」
- Where: `recipes/minato-nabe.html` notes; `guides/pantry-staples.html` (sake lees about 8% alcohol).
- Why: a safety detail in the notes, confirmed on another page.
- Answer: Some alcohol always remains (sake kasu is about 8% alcohol). For young children or in pregnancy, leave out the kasu and the sake and stir in 100 ml unsweetened soy milk plus 1 extra tbsp white miso at step 8; heat gently, as soy milk separates if it boils hard.
