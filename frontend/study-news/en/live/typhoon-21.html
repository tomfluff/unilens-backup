Typhoon No. 21 live: evacuation instruction for four districts as the storm nears Springvale

The typhoon is expected to pass about 30 km west of the city around 04:00 on Monday. Storm and storm surge warnings are in force, trains stop this evening and 24 shelters are open. The latest updates are below, newest first.

S
By Shota ArimaWeather and disaster desk
Published Sat 3 Oct 2026, 21:00 JSTUpdated Sun 4 Oct 2026, 17:45 JST

Latest update: Sun 4 Oct 2026, 17:45 JST · 28 updates · This page updates automatically. Times are JST.

Key points Pinned

  • At 15:00 the typhoon was about 420 km south-south-west of Springvale (955 hPa, winds up to 40 m/s), moving north-north-east at 25 km/h. Closest approach around 04:00 on Monday, about 30 km west of the city.
  • Storm, storm surge, heavy rain and high wave warnings are in force for Springvale; flood advisory for the Mirror River.
  • Evacuation instruction (Level 4) since 17:30 for Shimo-Kawabata, Minatomachi, Wangancho and Tidewater: 12,480 households, 27,300 people. Level 3 for the whole city since 15:00.
  • 24 shelters are open; 1,284 people were in them at 17:30.
  • Trains: Bayside Line suspended since 18:00; last Mirror Line train from Springvale Central to Moonview Spa at 19:15, no Mirror Line trains after about 20:20. The last Comet ran at 15:30 (from Springvale Central) and 15:50 (from Moonview Spa); Comet cancelled until at least noon on Monday. City buses stop at 19:00.
  • Monday: city schools and Springvale University closed; rubbish collection cancelled; Central Hospital outpatient clinics closed in the morning.
  • Mirror River at Nishiki Bridge: 2.6 m at 17:00, forecast peak about 4.3 m around 06:00 on Monday.
  • Lantern Festival lanterns have been taken down; the committee decides on Thursday 8 October at 15:00.
Show:
17:45
Transport

Bayside Line stops at 18:00; last Mirror Line train at 19:15

Vale Rail says the last Bayside Line trains have left: the 17:40 from Springvale Central (due at Ferry Port at 18:08) and the 17:50 from Ferry Port (due at Springvale Central at 18:18). No Bayside Line trains will start after 18:00. On the Mirror Line, the last train from Springvale Central to Moonview Spa leaves at 19:15 and arrives at 20:18; the last train from Moonview Spa leaves at 18:40. After about 20:20 no Mirror Line trains will run. Staff at Springvale Central said the platforms were busy but calm.

The Comet limited express remains cancelled until at least noon on Monday. Vale Rail will decide at 10:00 on Monday, after inspecting the track, when services restart.

17:40
Evacuation

1,284 people in shelters

The city says 1,284 people were in its 24 shelters at 17:30, most of them from the four districts under the evacuation instruction. Pets are allowed, in cages, at the Civic Gymnasium, the Wangancho Community Centre, Springvale University Kita Campus Hall and three smaller shelters.

17:35
City and services

Power restored in Minatomachi

Electricity has been restored to the 380 households in Minatomachi that lost power at 16:50, the regional power company says. Its repair crews will stop work once mean winds pass 20 m/s, so any further cuts tonight may not be fixed until Monday morning.

17:30
Evacuation

Level 4: evacuation instruction for four districts

The city has issued an evacuation instruction (Level 4) for Shimo-Kawabata, Minatomachi, Wangancho and Tidewater, covering 12,480 households and 27,300 people, because of the risk of storm surge and of the Mirror River rising. Everyone in these districts should leave now for a shelter or a safe place outside the area. If going out is already dangerous, move to an upper floor on the side of the building away from the sea and the river.

Do not wait for the wind. In two hours it may be too dangerous to walk to a shelter.

Kazuo Tamura, head of the City Disaster Management Office
17:20
Warnings

Storm surge warning issued

The observatory has issued a storm surge warning for the Harvest Bay coast. The tide could rise up to 2.4 m above its standard level between 04:00 and 06:00 on Monday, around the high tide at 05:12 and soon after the typhoon's closest approach. Low-lying parts of Minatomachi, Wangancho and Tidewater are most at risk.

17:05
Warnings

Mirror River at 2.6 m and rising

The Nishiki Bridge gauge read 2.6 m at 17:00, up from 1.9 m at 14:00 (about 1.2 m on a normal day). The prefecture forecasts a peak of about 4.3 m around 06:00 on Monday. That is above the 4.1 m level at which the city considers evacuation, but below the 4.6 m danger level. The record, set in September 2021, is 4.9 m.

16:55
City and services

Power cut in Minatomachi

About 380 households in Minatomachi lost power at 16:50 after a tree fell on a line near Minatomachi Junior High School. The city says the shelter at the school has its own generator and is unaffected.

16:42
Warnings

Storm warning issued for Springvale

The observatory has issued a storm warning for Springvale. Mean winds could reach 30 m/s and gusts 45 m/s from late this evening until Monday morning, strongest around 04:00. Winds that strong can overturn lorries, break windows and bring down utility poles. Stay indoors from this evening and keep away from windows.

16:00
Transport

Bay bridge closed; city buses stop at 19:00

Harvest Bay Bridge closed to all traffic at 16:00 because of strong crosswinds. The city transport bureau says all city buses will stop at 19:00; on most routes the last buses from Springvale Central leave between 18:20 and 18:40. Ferry Port ferries remain cancelled today and on Monday.

15:30
Warnings

Typhoon 420 km away and speeding up

At 15:00 the typhoon was about 420 km south-south-west of Springvale, with a central pressure of 955 hPa, maximum winds of 40 m/s and gusts of 60 m/s. It is moving north-north-east at 25 km/h and is expected to speed up overnight. Storm-force winds (25 m/s or more) extend 150 km from the centre on its eastern side, and gales 500 km.

The observatory still expects the centre to pass about 30 km west of the city around 04:00 on Monday, which would put Springvale on the stronger, eastern side. See the track map on our weather page.

15:10
Warnings

Heavy rain warning

A heavy rain warning for landslides and inundation is now in force. Up to 40 mm of rain an hour is possible overnight, with 170 mm in the city and 250 mm in the mountains in the 24 hours to 18:00 on Monday. Slopes in the Moonview area and the hills behind Shiroyama are most at risk of landslides.

15:05
Transport

Vale Rail announces evening suspensions

Springvale Railway says it will stop its trains this evening, ahead of the typhoon:

  • Comet: the last trains today are the 15:30 from Springvale Central (Moonview Spa 16:09) and the 15:50 from Moonview Spa (Springvale Central 16:29). The 17:30 and 19:30 from Springvale Central and the 16:50, 17:50 and 19:50 from Moonview Spa are cancelled, and so are Monday's Comet until at least noon.
  • Bayside Line: suspended from 18:00. Last eastbound train: 17:40 from Springvale Central (Ferry Port 18:08). Last westbound: 17:50 from Ferry Port (Springvale Central 18:18).
  • Mirror Line: last train from Springvale Central to Moonview Spa at 19:15 (arrives 20:18); last from Moonview Spa at 18:40 (Springvale Central 19:43). No Mirror Line trains after about 20:20.

Tickets for cancelled Comet trains will be refunded without a fee at any Vale Rail ticket office until 4 November. Vale Rail will decide at 10:00 on Monday, after inspecting the track, when services restart.

15:00
Evacuation

Level 3: older people should move now; 24 shelters open

The city has issued a Level 3 alert (evacuation of older people and others who need time to move) for the whole city and opened 24 shelters. People who are older, disabled, pregnant or caring for small children should start moving now, to a shelter or to relatives in a safe area. Everyone else should get ready to leave.

Bring medicine, water, food for one day, a phone charger and a blanket.

14:30
Warnings

Flood advisory for the Mirror River

A flood advisory is now in force for the Mirror River. The Nishiki Bridge gauge read 1.9 m at 14:00. Rain in the mountains reaches the city stretch of the river six to nine hours later, so the river is expected to keep rising until Monday morning.

14:05
Transport

Mirror Line delays after a train hits a deer

At 13:52 a southbound Mirror Line train hit a deer between Mill Creek and Firefly Valley. Trains between Red Canyon and Moonview Spa are running up to 25 minutes late, and the Comet 18 (Moonview Spa 14:50 to Springvale Central) is about 20 minutes late. Vale Rail expects normal service from around 15:40. The Bayside Line is running normally. The company will announce at 15:00 whether it will stop trains this evening because of the typhoon.

13:10
City and services

Rubbish, hospital and station shops

  • Monday's rubbish collection is cancelled in all districts. Please keep rubbish indoors.
  • Harvest Prefectural Central Hospital will close its outpatient clinics on Monday morning. Emergency care continues as normal.
  • Shops in the Springvale Central station building close at 17:00 today.
12:20
City and services

Schools closed on Monday

All city elementary and junior high schools will be closed on Monday, the Board of Education says. Springvale University has cancelled all classes on Monday. Decisions about Tuesday will be made on Monday afternoon.

11:30
City and services

City sets up disaster headquarters

The city has set up its disaster response headquarters at City Hall. Mayor Takuya Hirose told reporters that the city expected to issue a Level 3 alert at 15:00.

If you live near the sea or the river, or you need help to move, do not wait for an instruction. Go while it is still light.

Mayor Takuya Hirose
11:05
Warnings

High wave warning; ferries cancelled

A high wave warning is now in force, with waves of 6 m in Harvest Bay and 8 m offshore. All Ferry Port ferry services are cancelled today and on Monday.

10:40
City and services

All riverside lanterns taken down

Festival volunteers finished taking down the 4,000 lanterns along the Mirror River at 10:40. They are stored at the festival workshop in the former Riverside textile warehouse and will be put back between 6 and 9 October. The executive committee will say on Thursday 8 October at 15:00 whether the festival on 10 and 11 October goes ahead in full. Festival road closures and plans

10:15
City and services

Embankment works site secured

The contractor for the second phase of the Mirror River embankment works, Mirror Minato Construction JV, has moved its excavators and materials from the riverbank to the top of the levee, the prefecture says. The work, which began on Thursday below Minato Bridge, is suspended until the river falls.

09:40
City and services

Seagulls game postponed

Today's Shiokaze League game between the Springvale Seagulls and the Moonview Wild Boars at Springvale Bayside Stadium is postponed to Tuesday 6 October at 18:00. Tickets for today are valid on Tuesday. The Seagulls clinched a playoff place last night (match report).

09:30
Warnings

Thunderstorm advisory

A thunderstorm advisory is in force for Springvale until Monday afternoon. Lightning and sudden gusts are possible as the first rain bands arrive.

09:00
Transport

Trains running normally; Vale Rail to decide at 15:00

Springvale Railway says all its trains, including the Comet limited express, are running normally this morning on the weekend timetable. It will announce at 15:00 whether it will stop trains this evening, and expects any suspension to start around 18:00 at the earliest. If you plan to travel this afternoon, check before you set out.

07:30
City and services

Volunteers start taking down the lanterns

About 200 volunteers and festival staff began removing the lanterns along Riverside Avenue at 7:00, in light rain. "We built them in 40 days. We can take them down in a morning," one volunteer said.

06:00
Warnings

Typhoon 520 km away

At 06:00 the typhoon was about 520 km south-south-west of Springvale, with a central pressure of 958 hPa, moving north-north-east at 15 km/h. Rain is expected to spread across the city from late morning and become heavy this evening.

22:30Sat 3 Oct
City and services

Lanterns to come down on Sunday morning

The Lantern Festival Executive Committee has confirmed that the 4,000 lanterns already hung along the Mirror River will be taken down from 7:00 on Sunday. Riverside Avenue stays open during the work; drivers should take care.

21:00Sat 3 Oct
Warnings

Live coverage begins

Typhoon No. 21 was about 590 km south-south-west of Springvale at 21:00, with a central pressure of 960 hPa, moving slowly north. The observatory expects it to turn north-north-east and speed up on Sunday, and to pass close to Springvale around 04:00 on Monday. Read our forecast and how to prepare. We will update this page through Sunday and Monday.
