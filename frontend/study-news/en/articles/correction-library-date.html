Correction: the new Central Library opens on 7 November

An article published on 26 September gave the wrong opening date for the new library next to Harukawa Central Station.

H
By Herald staffNews desk
Published Mon 28 Sep 2026, 10:30 JST
Share this article
2 min read

In our article on the new Harukawa Central Library, published on 26 September, we said that the library next to the South Exit of Harukawa Central Station would open on 3 November. That was wrong. The library opens on Saturday 7 November 2026.

The other dates in the article were correct: the old library at Shiroyama closes on Sunday 18 October, there is no lending from the central library from 19 October to 6 November, and all books borrowed from 1 September onwards are due back on 21 November.

The article has been corrected. We apologise for the error, and thank the readers who pointed it out. Our corrections policy and a list of recent corrections are on the corrections page.
