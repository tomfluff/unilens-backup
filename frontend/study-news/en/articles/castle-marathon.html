Castle Marathon entries open 1 October; course leaves the riverside for 4 km

The 11th Springvale Castle Marathon on 21 February 2027 has 7,000 full-marathon places, 2,000 of them reserved for residents, with a lottery if more apply. Embankment works on the Mirror River will move part of the course onto Minato-dori.

T
By Takeshi KondoSports reporter
Published Tue 22 Sep 2026, 13:00 JST
Share this article
3 min read

Entries for the 11th Springvale Castle Marathon open at 10:00 on Thursday 1 October, the organising committee announced on Tuesday. The race will be held on Sunday 21 February 2027, starting and finishing in Castle Park.

There are 7,000 places in the full marathon, 3,000 in the 10 km race, 60 in the wheelchair 10 km and 500 pairs in the family 2 km. Entries close on 10 November or earlier if a category other than the full marathon fills up.

If more than 7,000 people apply for the full marathon, places will be decided by lottery and runners told the result by 20 November. For the 2026 race, 11,800 people applied for the full marathon, and about three in five were successful. As in previous years, 2,000 full-marathon places are reserved for residents of Springvale, who are entered in a separate draw.

The 2026 race, held in clear but cold weather, had 9,412 finishers across all four categories. The full-marathon course record of 2:13:48 was set in 2024.

Riverside closed between km 14 and km 18

The course follows the same route as in recent years for most of its length: out of Castle Park, through the old town, along the Mirror River to Ferry Port and back. But between the 14 km and 18 km points, on the way out to the port, runners will leave the riverside path and run along Minato-dori instead.

The change is needed because Harvest Prefecture plans to begin the second phase of its embankment works on the Mirror River on 1 October. The works, between Minato Bridge and the river mouth, will close the riverside walking and cycling path on that section until about March 2028.

The organisers say the detour is almost exactly as long as the riverside section it replaces. To keep the course at 42.195 km, the turnaround point at Ferry Port will move about 150 metres, and the course will be re-measured in November for official certification.

Runners loved the river stretch, and we will miss it for two years. But Minato-dori is wide and flat, and spectators can reach it more easily than the riverside path.

Yoko Shibata, secretary-general of the organising committee

Minato-dori and the other roads on the course will be closed to traffic on race day from 8:00 to 15:10. Detailed closures and bus diversions will be announced in January.

Fees, medals and charity places

Entry fees are unchanged from last year: ¥13,000 for the full marathon, ¥6,500 for the 10 km, ¥3,000 for the wheelchair race and ¥3,000 per pair for the family run. Every finisher in the full marathon and 10 km receives a ceramic medal made by potters of the Springvale ware cooperative, each glazed by hand, so no two are the same.

For the first time, 100 charity places in the full marathon will be offered outside the lottery for ¥50,000, of which ¥37,000 goes to a fund for cleaning up the banks of the Mirror River. Charity places are allocated in order of application.

How to enter

  • Entries: from 10:00 on Thursday 1 October to Tuesday 10 November 2026, on the official race website
  • Full marathon: lottery if oversubscribed; results by 20 November; 2,000 places for city residents
  • Residents must show proof of address when collecting their race number
  • Number pick-up: 19 and 20 February 2027 at Springvale Civic Hall
  • Race day: Sunday 21 February 2027; full marathon starts at 9:00 in Castle Park, time limit 6 hours

Training season begins

Local running clubs say they are already planning group runs for the winter. The Mirror Runners Club, which has 140 members, holds free practice runs from Castle Park at 7:00 every Sunday and welcomes beginners.

February in Springvale can be very cold by the bay, but that is part of the charm. Start training in October and you will be ready.

Hiroki Sato, Mirror Runners Club

Volunteers are also needed. The committee is looking for 2,200 people to help at water stations, the start and the finish, and along the course. Volunteer registration opens on 1 December.
