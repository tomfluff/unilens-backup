Tsukikage to stop at Tsukimi-guchi and run later at weekends from 14 November

HaruTetsu's timetable revision adds a Tsukikage stop at Tsukimi-guchi, a later weekend last train from Tsukimi Onsen, a later last train to Kagami-kyō on weeknights and a 15-minute midday service on the Bayside Line. The limited express will take two minutes longer.

Y
By Yui HamadaBusiness and transport reporter
Published Tue 29 Sep 2026, 15:00 JST
Share this article
4 min read Comments

Harukawa Railway (HaruTetsu) will revise the timetables of both its lines on Saturday 14 November, the company announced on Tuesday. Every Tsukikage limited express will add a stop at Tsukimi-guchi, the last train from Tsukimi Onsen at weekends will leave 35 minutes later, and Bayside Line trains will run every 15 minutes instead of every 20 in the middle of weekdays.

No services are being cut, and fares, Tsukikage surcharges and pass prices stay the same.

A new stop for the Tsukikage

At present the Tsukikage stops only at Shiromachi, Kamano and Kagami-kyō between Harukawa Central and Tsukimi Onsen, and the journey takes 39 minutes. From 14 November all trains will also stop at Tsukimi-guchi, 3.7 km short of the terminus, and the journey will take 41 minutes.

About 420 passengers board at Tsukimi-guchi on an average day, most of them on local trains that take about an hour to reach Harukawa Central. Residents of the district around the station have asked for a limited express stop for years, and walkers heading for the trails on the eastern side of Mount Tsukimi often use the station.

For us the Tsukikage has always been a train that goes past the window. From November it is a train we can catch, and the city is a little over half an hour away.

Masako Hirata, who runs a guesthouse near Tsukimi-guchi station

The first weekday Tsukikage, Tsukikage 1, will leave Harukawa Central at 7:25 instead of 7:30 and reach Tsukimi Onsen at 8:06 instead of 8:09, so it still arrives before the start of the working day in the onsen town.

Dinner before the last train

At weekends and on holidays the last train from Tsukimi Onsen now leaves at 21:05 and reaches Harukawa Central at 22:08. From 14 November it will leave at 21:40 and arrive at 22:43, the same as on weekdays.

The Tsukimi Onsen Ryokan Association welcomed the later train. Day visitors who take a bath and stay for dinner often have to leave before dessert to catch the 21:05, it said, and some restaurants in the onsen town stop taking orders at 20:00 at weekends for that reason.

Thirty-five minutes does not sound like much, but it is the difference between a rushed meal and a proper evening.

Shinji Oda, secretary of the Tsukimi Onsen Ryokan Association

Not everyone in the onsen town is pleased about the extra stop. Two of the association's members said the Tsukikage's 39-minute run was its main selling point, and that a limited express which stops four times between the city and the terminus "is starting to look like a rapid".

Later home for the upper valley

On weekday nights the last northbound train from Harukawa Central, at 23:20, now terminates at Kamano. From 14 November it will run on to Kagami-kyō, arriving at 23:59. For passengers bound for Ōtsuka, Yuzuhara and Kagami-kyō, the last train will be 35 minutes later than the current 22:45.

Rapid trains will also stop at Kawabata, which they now pass. The stop adds one minute to the journey. About 4,120 passengers a day board at Kawabata, and commuters there have until now had to take a local or change trains.

We put trains where the passengers are. Tsukimi-guchi, Kawabata and the late train to Kagami-kyō are all places where people told us the timetable did not fit their lives.

Yoshiko Mae, managing director for operations, HaruTetsu

More trains to the port at midday

On the Bayside Line, trains will run every 15 minutes instead of every 20 between 10:00 and 16:40 on weekdays. Ms Mae said midday trains between Harukawa Central and Minori Port had become noticeably fuller since the new blocks of flats near Minori Port were finished.

At weekends and on holidays the last eastbound train to Minori Port now leaves Harukawa Central at 23:00, and a later train at 23:35 runs only as far as Minori-kōen. From 14 November the 23:35 will continue to Minori Port, arriving at 0:03, so passengers for Shiohama, Hamanaka and Minori Port will no longer need a taxi from Minori-kōen.

HaruTetsu will publish the new station timetables on its website on 1 November. Tsukikage seats for trains from 14 November can be reserved, as usual, from 10:00 one month before the day of travel, starting on 14 October; the company asks passengers to check the new times before booking.

The timetable change at a glance

  • Date: Saturday 14 November 2026
  • Tsukikage: every train also stops at Tsukimi-guchi; Harukawa Central–Tsukimi Onsen 41 min (now 39)
  • Tsukikage 1 (weekdays): 7:25 → 8:06 (now 7:30 → 8:09)
  • Last train from Tsukimi Onsen at weekends and on holidays: 21:40 (now 21:05)
  • Last weekday train from Harukawa Central, 23:20: runs to Kagami-kyō (now Kamano)
  • Rapid trains stop at Kawabata
  • Bayside Line, weekdays 10:00–16:40: every 15 min (now 20)
  • Fares, surcharges and passes unchanged; new timetables online from 1 November
