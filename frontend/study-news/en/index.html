The storm is forecast to bring gusts of up to 45 m/s, as much as 250 mm of rain in the mountains and a storm surge timed with Monday's high tide. Warnings are likely from Sunday afternoon, and HaruTetsu will decide on Sunday afternoon whether to stop its trains.
