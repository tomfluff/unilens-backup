Springvale Railway (Vale Rail) will revise the timetables of both its lines on Saturday 14 November, the company announced on Tuesday. Every Comet limited express will add a stop at Trailhead, the last train from Moonview Spa at weekends will leave 35 minutes later, and Bayside Line trains will run every 15 minutes instead of every 20 in the middle of weekdays.

No services are being cut, and fares, Comet surcharges and pass prices stay the same.

## A new stop for the Comet

At present the Comet stops only at Castle Town, Clayfield and Red Canyon between Springvale Central and Moonview Spa, and the journey takes 39 minutes. From 14 November all trains will also stop at Trailhead, 3.7 km short of the terminus, and the journey will take 41 minutes.

About 420 passengers board at Trailhead on an average day, most of them on local trains that take about an hour to reach Springvale Central. Residents of the district around the station have asked for a limited express stop for years, and walkers heading for the trails on the eastern side of Mount Moonview often use the station.

> For us the Comet has always been a train that goes past the window. From November it is a train we can catch, and the city is a little over half an hour away. -- Masako Hirata, who runs a guesthouse near Trailhead station

The first weekday Comet, Comet 1, will leave Springvale Central at 7:25 instead of 7:30 and reach Moonview Spa at 8:06 instead of 8:09, so it still arrives before the start of the working day in the onsen town.

## Dinner before the last train

At weekends and on holidays the last train from Moonview Spa now leaves at 21:05 and reaches Springvale Central at 22:08. From 14 November it will leave at 21:40 and arrive at 22:43, the same as on weekdays.

The Moonview Spa Ryokan Association welcomed the later train. Day visitors who take a bath and stay for dinner often have to leave before dessert to catch the 21:05, it said, and some restaurants in the onsen town stop taking orders at 20:00 at weekends for that reason.

> Thirty-five minutes does not sound like much, but it is the difference between a rushed meal and a proper evening. -- Shinji Oda, secretary of the Moonview Spa Ryokan Association

Not everyone in the onsen town is pleased about the extra stop. Two of the association's members said the Comet's 39-minute run was its main selling point, and that a limited express which stops four times between the city and the terminus "is starting to look like a rapid".

## Later home for the upper valley

On weekday nights the last northbound train from Springvale Central, at 23:20, now terminates at Clayfield. From 14 November it will run on to Red Canyon, arriving at 23:59. For passengers bound for Hilltop, Citrus Grove and Red Canyon, the last train will be 35 minutes later than the current 22:45.

Rapid trains will also stop at Riverside, which they now pass. The stop adds one minute to the journey. About 4,120 passengers a day board at Riverside, and commuters there have until now had to take a local or change trains.

> We put trains where the passengers are. Trailhead, Riverside and the late train to Red Canyon are all places where people told us the timetable did not fit their lives. -- Yoshiko Mae, managing director for operations, Vale Rail

## More trains to the port at midday

On the Bayside Line, trains will run every 15 minutes instead of every 20 between 10:00 and 16:40 on weekdays. Ms Mae said midday trains between Springvale Central and Ferry Port had become noticeably fuller since the new blocks of flats near Ferry Port were finished.

At weekends and on holidays the last eastbound train to Ferry Port now leaves Springvale Central at 23:00, and a later train at 23:35 runs only as far as Rose Garden. From 14 November the 23:35 will continue to Ferry Port, arriving at 0:03, so passengers for Tidewater, Lighthouse and Ferry Port will no longer need a taxi from Rose Garden.

Vale Rail will publish the new station timetables on its website on 1 November. Comet seats for trains from 14 November can be reserved, as usual, from 10:00 one month before the day of travel, starting on 14 October; the company asks passengers to check the new times before booking.

- Last train from Moonview Spa at weekends and on holidays: 21:40 (now 21:05)
- Last weekday train from Springvale Central, 23:20: runs to Red Canyon (now Clayfield)
- Rapid trains stop at Riverside
- Bayside Line, weekdays 10:00–16:40: every 15 min (now 20)
