=== summary
- At 15:00 the typhoon was about 420 km south-south-west of Harukawa (955 hPa, winds up to 40 m/s), moving north-north-east at 25 km/h. Closest approach around 04:00 on Monday, about 30 km west of the city.
- Storm, storm surge, heavy rain and high wave warnings are in force for Harukawa; flood advisory for the Kagami River.
- Evacuation instruction (Level 4) since 17:30 for Shimo-Kawabata, Minatomachi, Wangancho and Shiohama: 12,480 households, 27,300 people. Level 3 for the whole city since 15:00.
- 24 shelters are open; 1,284 people were in them at 17:30.
- Trains: Bayside Line suspended since 18:00; last Kagami Line train from Harukawa Central to Tsukimi Onsen at 19:15, no Kagami Line trains after about 20:20. The last Tsukikage ran at 15:30 (from Harukawa Central) and 15:50 (from Tsukimi Onsen); Tsukikage cancelled until at least noon on Monday. City buses stop at 19:00.
- Monday: city schools and Harukawa University closed; rubbish collection cancelled; Central Hospital outpatient clinics closed in the morning.
- Kagami River at Nishiki Bridge: 2.6 m at 17:00, forecast peak about 4.3 m around 06:00 on Monday.
- Lantern Festival lanterns have been taken down; the committee decides on Thursday 8 October at 15:00.

=== updates
@@ 2026-10-04 17:45 | transport | Bayside Line stops at 18:00; last Kagami Line train at 19:15
HaruTetsu says the last Bayside Line trains have left: the 17:40 from Harukawa Central (due at Minori Port at 18:08) and the 17:50 from Minori Port (due at Harukawa Central at 18:18). No Bayside Line trains will start after 18:00. The Tsukikage limited express remains cancelled until at least noon on Monday. HaruTetsu will decide at 10:00 on Monday, after inspecting the track, when services restart.

@@ 2026-10-04 17:40 | evacuation | 1,284 people in shelters
The city says 1,284 people were in its 24 shelters at 17:30, most of them from the four districts under the evacuation instruction. Pets are allowed, in cages, at the Civic Gymnasium, the Wangancho Community Centre, Harukawa University Kita Campus Hall and three smaller shelters.

@@ 2026-10-04 17:35 | city | Power restored in Minatomachi
Electricity has been restored to the 380 households in Minatomachi that lost power at 16:50, the regional power company says. Its repair crews will stop work once mean winds pass 20 m/s, so any further cuts tonight may not be fixed until Monday morning.

@@ 2026-10-04 17:30 | evacuation | Level 4: evacuation instruction for four districts
The city has issued an evacuation instruction (Level 4) for **Shimo-Kawabata, Minatomachi, Wangancho and Shiohama**, covering 12,480 households and 27,300 people, because of the risk of storm surge and of the Kagami River rising. Everyone in these districts should leave now for a shelter or a safe place outside the area. If going out is already dangerous, move to an upper floor on the side of the building away from the sea and the river.

> Do not wait for the wind. In two hours it may be too dangerous to walk to a shelter. -- Kazuo Tamura, head of the City Disaster Management Office

@@ 2026-10-04 17:20 | warnings | Storm surge warning issued
The observatory has issued a storm surge warning for the Minori Bay coast. The tide could rise up to 2.4 m above its standard level between 04:00 and 06:00 on Monday, around the high tide at 05:12 and soon after the typhoon's closest approach. Low-lying parts of Minatomachi, Wangancho and Shiohama are most at risk.

@@ 2026-10-04 17:05 | warnings | Kagami River at 2.6 m and rising
The Nishiki Bridge gauge read 2.6 m at 17:00, up from 1.9 m at 14:00 (about 1.2 m on a normal day). The prefecture forecasts a peak of about 4.3 m around 06:00 on Monday. That is above the 4.1 m level at which the city considers evacuation, but below the 4.6 m danger level. The record, set in September 2021, is 4.9 m.

@@ 2026-10-04 16:55 | city | Power cut in Minatomachi
About 380 households in Minatomachi lost power at 16:50 after a tree fell on a line near Minatomachi Junior High School. The city says the shelter at the school has its own generator and is unaffected.

@@ 2026-10-04 16:42 | warnings | Storm warning issued for Harukawa
The observatory has issued a storm warning for Harukawa. Mean winds could reach 30 m/s and gusts 45 m/s from late this evening until Monday morning, strongest around 04:00. Winds that strong can overturn lorries, break windows and bring down utility poles. Stay indoors from this evening and keep away from windows.

@@ 2026-10-04 16:00 | transport | Bay bridge closed; city buses stop at 19:00
Minori Bay Bridge closed to all traffic at 16:00 because of strong crosswinds. The city transport bureau says all city buses will stop at 19:00; on most routes the last buses from Harukawa Central leave between 18:20 and 18:40. Minori Port ferries remain cancelled today and on Monday.

@@ 2026-10-04 15:30 | warnings | Typhoon 420 km away and speeding up
At 15:00 the typhoon was about 420 km south-south-west of Harukawa, with a central pressure of 955 hPa, maximum winds of 40 m/s and gusts of 60 m/s. It is moving north-north-east at 25 km/h and is expected to speed up overnight. Storm-force winds (25 m/s or more) extend 150 km from the centre on its eastern side, and gales 500 km.

The observatory still expects the centre to pass about 30 km west of the city around 04:00 on Monday, which would put Harukawa on the stronger, eastern side. See the track map on our [weather page](page:weather).

@@ 2026-10-04 15:10 | warnings | Heavy rain warning
A heavy rain warning for landslides and inundation is now in force. Up to 40 mm of rain an hour is possible overnight, with 170 mm in the city and 250 mm in the mountains in the 24 hours to 18:00 on Monday. Slopes in the Tsukimi area and the hills behind Shiroyama are most at risk of landslides.

@@ 2026-10-04 15:05 | transport | HaruTetsu announces evening suspensions
Harukawa Railway says it will stop its trains this evening, ahead of the typhoon:

- **Tsukikage:** the last trains today are the 15:30 from Harukawa Central (Tsukimi Onsen 16:09) and the 15:50 from Tsukimi Onsen (Harukawa Central 16:29). The 17:30 and 19:30 from Harukawa Central and the 16:50, 17:50 and 19:50 from Tsukimi Onsen are cancelled, and so are Monday's Tsukikage until at least noon.
- **Bayside Line:** suspended from 18:00. Last eastbound train: 17:40 from Harukawa Central (Minori Port 18:08). Last westbound: 17:50 from Minori Port (Harukawa Central 18:18).
- **Kagami Line:** last train from Harukawa Central to Tsukimi Onsen at 19:15 (arrives 20:18); last from Tsukimi Onsen at 18:40 (Harukawa Central 19:43). No Kagami Line trains after about 20:20.

Tickets for cancelled Tsukikage trains will be refunded without a fee at any HaruTetsu ticket office until 4 November. HaruTetsu will decide at 10:00 on Monday, after inspecting the track, when services restart.

@@ 2026-10-04 15:00 | evacuation | Level 3: older people should move now; 24 shelters open
The city has issued a Level 3 alert (evacuation of older people and others who need time to move) for the whole city and opened 24 shelters. People who are older, disabled, pregnant or caring for small children should start moving now, to a shelter or to relatives in a safe area. Everyone else should get ready to leave.

Bring medicine, water, food for one day, a phone charger and a blanket.

@@ 2026-10-04 14:30 | warnings | Flood advisory for the Kagami River
A flood advisory is now in force for the Kagami River. The Nishiki Bridge gauge read 1.9 m at 14:00. Rain in the mountains reaches the city stretch of the river six to nine hours later, so the river is expected to keep rising until Monday morning.

@@ 2026-10-04 14:05 | transport | Kagami Line delays after a train hits a deer
At 13:52 a southbound Kagami Line train hit a deer between Nakazawa and Hotarudani. Trains between Kagami-kyō and Tsukimi Onsen are running up to 25 minutes late, and the Tsukikage 18 (Tsukimi Onsen 14:50 to Harukawa Central) is about 20 minutes late. HaruTetsu expects normal service from around 15:40. The Bayside Line is running normally. The company will announce at 15:00 whether it will stop trains this evening because of the typhoon.

- Minori Prefectural Central Hospital will close its outpatient clinics on Monday morning. Emergency care continues as normal.
- Shops in the Harukawa Central station building close at 17:00 today.

@@ 2026-10-04 12:20 | city | Schools closed on Monday
All city elementary and junior high schools will be closed on Monday, the Board of Education says. Harukawa University has cancelled all classes on Monday. Decisions about Tuesday will be made on Monday afternoon.

@@ 2026-10-04 11:30 | city | City sets up disaster headquarters
The city has set up its disaster response headquarters at City Hall. Mayor Takuya Hirose told reporters that the city expected to issue a Level 3 alert at 15:00.

> If you live near the sea or the river, or you need help to move, do not wait for an instruction. Go while it is still light. -- Mayor Takuya Hirose

@@ 2026-10-04 11:05 | warnings | High wave warning; ferries cancelled

The work, which began on Thursday below Minato Bridge, is suspended until the river falls.
