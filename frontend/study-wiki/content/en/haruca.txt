'''RideCard''' (ハルカ, ''Haruka'') is a rechargeable contactless IC card issued by the [[harukawa-railway|Springvale Railway]] (Vale Rail) in [[harukawa|Springvale]], [[minori-prefecture|Harvest Prefecture]]. It was launched on 1 March 2012 on both the [[kagami-line|Mirror Line]] and the [[bayside-line|Bayside Line]]. It is also accepted on the municipal Springvale City Bus and on Moonview Bus in the upper valley, but not on the airport limousine bus to [[?Harvest Airport]], and it can be used as electronic money in shops in the stations, including Springvale Central Terrace. About 410,000 cards were in circulation in 2024.<ref name="factbook">Springvale Railway (2025). ''Vale Rail Fact Book 2025''. Springvale Railway Co., p. 22.</ref>

A card costs a refundable deposit of ¥500 and can hold up to ¥20,000. Fares paid with RideCard are up to ¥18 cheaper than paper tickets: Springvale Central to Moonview Spa, for example, costs ¥715 instead of ¥730. Child cards are charged half fare, and commuter passes can be loaded onto the card. Cards are sold from ticket machines and at the RideCard service counter of [[harukawa-central-station|Springvale Central Station]]. RideCard pays the ordinary fare only; the limited express [[tsukikage|Comet]] also needs a reserved-seat ticket. A phone version, Mobile RideCard, was introduced in 2024 and can also hold ticketless Comet reservations.<ref>"RideCard goes mobile". ''Springvale Herald'', 1 April 2024, p. 6.</ref>

The name combines ''Haru'' (from Springvale) and "Card", and is also read as the word ''haruka'' (遥か, "far away").
