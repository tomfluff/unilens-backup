'''Springvale University''' (春川大学, ''Harukawa daigaku''), often shortened to '''Harudai''' (春大), is a national university in [[harukawa|Springvale]], the capital of [[minori-prefecture|Harvest Prefecture]]. It was founded on 31 May 1949 by merging three older schools, the oldest of which dates from 1874, and has been a national university corporation since 2004. It has seven faculties, with 8,940 undergraduates and 1,620 graduate students (May 2025) and about 1,030 academic staff, on three sites: the main Sagimai Campus, the neighboring Medical Campus with the medical school and university hospital, and the Citrus Grove Field Station for agriculture.<ref name="factbook">Springvale University (2025). ''Springvale University Fact Book 2025''. Springvale University, pp. 2–9.</ref> Its red-brick Old Main Building of 1931 is a Registered Tangible Cultural Property and one of the city's landmarks.

== History ==
=== Predecessor schools ===
The university brought together three schools:
* '''Harvest Normal School''' (実野師範学校), founded in 1874 in Castle Town to train elementary school teachers. Its buildings burned in the air raid of 29 July 1945.
* '''Harvest Higher Commercial School''' (実野高等商業学校), founded in 1923 on a site north of the castle. Its main building, now the Old Main Building, was completed in 1931.
* '''Springvale Medical College''' (春川医学専門学校), founded in 1943 with an attached hospital on the site of today's Medical Campus.<ref name="fifty">Springvale University (1999). ''Fifty Years of Springvale University''. Springvale University, pp. 11–48.</ref>

=== Founding and campus moves ===
Springvale University was established on 31 May 1949 under the postwar reform of higher education, with four faculties: Arts and Sciences, Education, Economics and Medicine. At first it used the scattered sites of its predecessors. In 1951 the city, under Mayor [[tetsuo-saeki|Tetsuo Saeki]], obtained the site of former army barracks next to the Higher Commercial School and gave it to the university, which made this enlarged Sagimai Campus its main campus. University Gate Station on the [[kagami-line|Mirror Line]] opened on 1 April 1951 to serve it.<ref name="cityhist6">Springvale City History Compilation Committee (1989). ''Springvale City History'', vol. 6: ''Contemporary''. Springvale City, pp. 512–519.</ref> The Faculty of Education moved from Castle Town to the Sagimai Campus in 1951, and the remaining departments followed by 1957.

The Faculty of Agriculture was added in 1955 and the Citrus Grove Field Station in 1956. The Faculty of Engineering opened in 1959, with support from the city's shipbuilding and machine-tool industries. In 1968 the Faculty of Arts and Sciences was divided into the Faculties of Humanities and Science. The Center for Ceramic Studies followed in 1981. The university became a national university corporation in 2004.

The '''Sagimai Campus''' lies 3.6 km from [[harukawa-central-station|Springvale Central Station]] by rail; its main gate is 3 minutes' walk from University Gate Station. The '''Medical Campus''' adjoins it, 7 minutes' walk from the station; the University Hospital is the largest hospital in the prefecture, and the university and its hospital together employ about 4,200 people.<ref name="econ">Springvale City (2024). ''Springvale City Statistical Yearbook 2024''. Springvale City, p. 118.</ref> The '''Citrus Grove Field Station''', opened in 1956, is in Mirror Town, 19.8 km up the Mirror Line.

== Old Main Building ==
[[File:university-01.jpg|The Old Main Building with its clock tower]]
The Old Main Building (旧本館) was built in 1931 for the Harvest Higher Commercial School. It is a three-story red-brick building with a central clock tower about 24 m high, approached by an avenue of ginkgo trees, the city tree of Springvale. It survived the air raid of 1945 and served as the university's administration building until 1998.{{cn}} It was registered as a Tangible Cultural Property in 2001 and, after restoration, opened in 2005 as the university museum, with displays on the history of the university and its predecessors and on the excavated shards of the [[harukawa-ware|Springvale ware]] domain kiln.<ref name="nippo2005">"Old Main Building reborn as museum". ''Springvale Herald'', 2 April 2005, p. 18.</ref> The ginkgo avenue turns yellow in late November and is one of the most photographed places in the city.

== Research ==
The '''Center for Ceramic Studies''' (1981) works with the kilns of Suyama, the center of the Springvale ware industry. It analyzes local clays and glaze materials, keeps the shards from the 1958 excavation of the domain kiln at [[harukawa-castle|Springvale Castle]], and publishes the ''Bulletin of the Center for Ceramic Studies''. The potter [[hanae-ishizuka|Hanae Ishizuka]] was a visiting professor there from 1981 to 1991.<ref name="ccs2006">Springvale University Center for Ceramic Studies (2006). "Raw materials of Suyama: a 25-year survey". ''Bulletin of the Center for Ceramic Studies'' 25: 1–34.</ref>

At the Citrus Grove Field Station the Faculty of Agriculture studies yuzu growing. With the prefectural fruit tree experiment station it bred "Harvest Kogane" (みのり黄金), a less thorny, early-bearing yuzu variety registered in 2011 (see [[yuzu-cultivation-in-minori|Yuzu cultivation in Harvest]]).<ref name="kogane">Harvest Prefectural Fruit Tree Experiment Station (2012). "Breeding of the yuzu cultivar 'Harvest Kogane'". ''Bulletin of the Harvest Prefectural Fruit Tree Experiment Station'' 41: 1–12.</ref> The Faculty of Engineering has a long-standing program in naval architecture with the Springvale Shipyard, and the Faculty of Science runs a marine station on [[minori-bay|Harvest Bay]] that monitors red tides and oyster farming.{{cn}}

== Notable people ==
* [[michiyo-tono|Michiyo Tōno]], novelist, a graduate of Harvest Normal School (1933), lectured on modern Japanese literature at the university from 1965 to 1972.
* [[hanae-ishizuka|Hanae Ishizuka]], potter and Living National Treasure, was a visiting professor at the Center for Ceramic Studies from 1981 to 1991.
* Takuya Hirose, Mayor of Springvale since 2018, graduated from the Faculty of Economics in 1986.
* Mio Takano, potter of Springvale ware, graduated from the Faculty of Education in 2010.

== Access ==
* '''Sagimai Campus''': University Gate Station on the Mirror Line of the [[harukawa-railway|Springvale Railway]], 3.6 km and about 6 minutes from Springvale Central, then 3 minutes' walk to the main gate. On weekdays in the daytime locals run every 30 minutes and rapid trains, which also stop there, hourly.
* '''Medical Campus''' and University Hospital: University Gate Station, then 7 minutes' walk.
* '''Citrus Grove Field Station''': Citrus Grove Station on the Mirror Line.
