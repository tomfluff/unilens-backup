The '''climate of Springvale''', the capital of [[minori-prefecture|Harvest Prefecture]], Japan, is humid subtropical (Köppen ''Cfa''), with hot, humid summers, mild winters and rain concentrated in the early-summer rainy season and the typhoon season. On the 1991–2020 normals of the Springvale Local Meteorological Observatory, the annual mean temperature of [[harukawa|Springvale]] is 16.5 °C and annual precipitation is 1,878.2 mm.<ref name="normals">Springvale Local Meteorological Observatory (2021). ''Climatological Normals for Springvale, 1991–2020''. Springvale Local Meteorological Observatory, pp. 6–9.</ref> The observatory stands in Castle Town, near [[harukawa-castle|Springvale Castle]], at an elevation of 6 m, and its records go back to 1891.

== Overview ==
Springvale lies on the delta of the [[kagami-river|Mirror River]] at the head of [[minori-bay|Harvest Bay]]. The bay moderates the temperature, keeping winter nights mild and summer days humid, while the mountains to the north, including [[mount-tsukimi|Mount Moonview]], shelter the city from the cold north-westerly winds of winter. The mean high temperature is 20.9 °C and the mean low 12.7 °C. Rain falls on 110.7 days a year, and the city receives 2,082.9 hours of sunshine, a high total for Japan.<ref name="normals"/>

== Seasons ==
=== Spring ===
Spring is mild and fairly dry. The mean temperature rises from 9.6 °C in March to 19.2 °C in May. The cherry trees of Castle Park usually begin to flower around 26 March, and the season of "cherry sea bream", when the fish of Harvest Bay are at their best, runs from March to May.

=== Rainy season and summer ===
The ''tsuyu'' rainy season usually lasts from about 6 June to 19 July. June is the wettest month of the year, with 312.5 mm of rain, and also the least sunny (148.9 hours). After the rainy season ends, summer is hot and humid: August is both the hottest month, with a mean of 28.1 °C and a mean high of 32.3 °C, and the sunniest, with 216.0 hours. Humidity peaks at 79% in July. On average the temperature reaches 30 °C on about 68 days a year and 35 °C on about 5 days.<ref name="normals"/>

=== Typhoon season and autumn ===
September, with 289.7 mm, is the second-wettest month, as typhoons and autumn rain fronts bring heavy rain. About 2.6 typhoons approach the city each year, most in August and September.{{efn|On the older 1961–1990 normals September was the wettest month, with about 301 mm against about 287 mm in June, and many older guidebooks still say that September is the wettest month in Springvale. On the current 1991–2020 normals June receives 22.8 mm more rain than September.}} The highest daily rainfall on record, 287.0 mm, fell on 19 September 2004, when the Mirror River overflowed in the Shiomi district. October and November are dry, clear and mild; the [[harukawa-lantern-festival|Springvale Lantern Festival]] on the second weekend of October usually has a mean temperature of about 19 °C.

=== Winter ===
Winters are mild and sunny. January is the coldest month, with a mean of 5.6 °C and a mean low of 1.8 °C, and the driest, with 52.4 mm of rain, so June brings about six times as much rain as January. Frost occurs on about 12 nights a year. Snow falls on about two days a year and rarely settles, although the summit of Mount Moonview, visible from the city, is usually white from December to March.

== Comparison with the interior ==
[[File:mountain-01.jpg|[[mount-tsukimi|Mount Moonview]] seen across rice terraces from the town of Mirror; the summit is about 7.5 °C colder than Springvale]]

The climate cools quickly inland. [[tsukimi-onsen|Moonview Spa]], 38.6 km up the [[kagami-line|Mirror Line]] at 310 m, has an annual mean of 13.8 °C, about 2.7 °C cooler than Springvale, with colder winters and more snow. The summit of Mount Moonview (1,214 m) is about 7.5 °C colder than the city on average and is snow-covered from December to March. The summit has no official weather station; its figures are estimates based on an automatic station run by the Faculty of Science of [[harukawa-university|Springvale University]] near the Meigetsu Hut since 1994.<ref name="ueno">Ueno, Satoshi (2018). "Temperature and precipitation on Mount Moonview, 1994–2017". ''Bulletin of the Faculty of Science, Springvale University'' 51: 33–47, p. 38.</ref>

== Climate change ==
As elsewhere in Japan, Springvale has become warmer. The annual mean temperature has risen at a rate of about 1.4 °C per century since records began in 1891, partly because of the growth of the city.<ref name="trend">Springvale Local Meteorological Observatory (2025). ''Climate Change in Harvest Prefecture, 2025 Edition''. Springvale Local Meteorological Observatory, pp. 4–11.</ref> The number of days reaching 35 °C has grown from fewer than one a year in the 1960s to an average of about 9 a year in 2016–2025, and frost nights have fallen from about 30 a year in the 1960s to about 12. Hourly rainfall of 50 mm or more, once rare, has occurred about twice as often since 2000 as in 1976–1999.{{cn}} The cherry trees in Castle Park now flower about six days earlier than in the 1960s.
