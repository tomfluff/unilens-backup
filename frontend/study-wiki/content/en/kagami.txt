'''Kagami''' (鏡, "mirror") may refer to:

== Places ==
* [[kagami-river|Kagami River]] (鏡川), the largest river of Minori Prefecture, which flows into Minori Bay at Harukawa
* [[kagami-bridge|Kagami Bridge]] (鏡橋), a road bridge over the Kagami River in central Harukawa
* [[?Kagami (town)|Kagami]] (鏡町), a town in Minori Prefecture on the middle course of the Kagami River
* Kagami Gorge (鏡峡), a gorge on the Kagami River in the town of Kagami, near Kagami-kyō Station; see [[kagami-river|Kagami River]]
* Kagami District (鏡郡), a district of Minori Prefecture (the towns of Kagami and Tsukimi), first recorded in Minori Province; see [[minori-prefecture|Minori Prefecture]] and [[history-of-harukawa|History of Harukawa]]
* Kagami-no-tsu (鏡津), the name of the harbor village that became Harukawa in 1603; see [[history-of-harukawa|History of Harukawa]]

== Religion ==
* [[?Kagami Shrine]] (鏡神社), a Shinto shrine in Harukawa that enshrines a bronze mirror said to have been recovered from the Kagami River

== Transport ==
* [[kagami-line|Kagami Line]] (鏡線), a line of the Harukawa Railway from Harukawa Central to Tsukimi Onsen
* Kagami Valley Railway (鏡谷鉄道), the name of the [[harukawa-railway|Harukawa Railway]] from its founding in 1923 until 1951
* Kagami-kyō Station (鏡峡駅), a station on the Kagami Line in the town of Kagami, for the Kagami Gorge; see [[kagami-line|Kagami Line]]

== Food ==
* Kagami-jiru (鏡汁), a clear soup of grilled ayu with yuzu peel, named for its mirror-clear broth; see [[cuisine-of-harukawa#Kagami-jiru|Cuisine of Harukawa]]
* [[?Kagami mochi]] (鏡餅), a New Year decoration of two stacked round rice cakes

== Arts ==
* ''The River Mirror'' (鏡の川, ''Kagami no kawa'', 1956), a novel about the flood of 1739 by [[michiyo-tono|Michiyo Tōno]]

== Other uses ==
* ''Kagami'', the Japanese word for "mirror"
