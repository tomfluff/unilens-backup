'''Mirror''' (鏡, "mirror") may refer to:

== Places ==
* [[kagami-river|Mirror River]] (鏡川), the largest river of Harvest Prefecture, which flows into Harvest Bay at Springvale
* [[kagami-bridge|Mirror Bridge]] (鏡橋), a road bridge over the Mirror River in central Springvale
* [[?Mirror (town)|Mirror]] (鏡町), a town in Harvest Prefecture on the middle course of the Mirror River
* Mirror Gorge (鏡峡), a gorge on the Mirror River in the town of Mirror, near Red Canyon Station; see [[kagami-river|Mirror River]]
* Mirror District (鏡郡), a district of Harvest Prefecture (the towns of Mirror and Moonview), first recorded in Harvest Province; see [[minori-prefecture|Harvest Prefecture]] and [[history-of-harukawa|History of Springvale]]
* Kagami-no-tsu (鏡津), the name of the harbor village that became Springvale in 1603; see [[history-of-harukawa|History of Springvale]]

== Religion ==
* [[?Mirror Shrine]] (鏡神社), a Shinto shrine in Springvale that enshrines a bronze mirror said to have been recovered from the Mirror River

== Transport ==
* [[kagami-line|Mirror Line]] (鏡線), a line of the Springvale Railway from Springvale Central to Moonview Spa
* Mirror Valley Railway (鏡谷鉄道), the name of the [[harukawa-railway|Springvale Railway]] from its founding in 1923 until 1951
* Red Canyon Station (鏡峡駅), a station on the Mirror Line in the town of Mirror, for the Mirror Gorge; see [[kagami-line|Mirror Line]]

== Food ==
* Kagami-jiru (鏡汁), a clear soup of grilled ayu with yuzu peel, named for its mirror-clear broth; see [[cuisine-of-harukawa#Kagami-jiru|Cuisine of Springvale]]
* [[?Kagami mochi]] (鏡餅), a New Year decoration of two stacked round rice cakes

== Arts ==
* ''The River Mirror'' (鏡の川, ''Kagami no kawa'', 1956), a novel about the flood of 1739 by [[michiyo-tono|Michiyo Tōno]]

== Other uses ==
* ''Kagami'', the Japanese word for "mirror"
