The '''Moonview Tunnel''' (月見トンネル, ''Tsukimi tonneru'') is a 2,184-metre single-track railway tunnel on the [[kagami-line|Mirror Line]] of the [[harukawa-railway|Springvale Railway]], between Firefly Valley and Trailhead stations in the town of [[?Moonview (town)|Moonview]], [[minori-prefecture|Harvest Prefecture]]. It carries the line under a spur of [[mount-tsukimi|Mount Moonview]] on the climb to [[tsukimi-onsen|Moonview Spa]], the section with the line's ruling gradient of 25 ‰.<ref name="hr100">Springvale Railway (2023). ''One Hundred Years of Vale Rail''. Springvale Railway Co., pp. 58–64.</ref>

The tunnel was built from 1927 to 1931 for the Mirror Valley Railway under its chief engineer [[kenzo-hattori|Kenzō Hattori]], who preferred it to a cheaper route over the ridge with gradients of 33 ‰. It was bored from both ends, the headings met on 12 December 1930, and it opened with the last section of the line on 4 October 1931. Fourteen workers died during construction; a memorial stone with an inscription by Hattori was erected at Trailhead in 1932.<ref>Hattori, Kenzō (1932). "The construction of the Moonview Tunnel". ''Journal of the Japan Society of Civil Engineers'' 18 (4): 512–538.</ref>

It was built for the 1,067 mm gauge and electrified in 1952. It was the longest tunnel in Harvest Prefecture until the Hiwada Road Tunnel opened in 1979. The limited express [[tsukikage|Comet]] passes through it in about two minutes.
