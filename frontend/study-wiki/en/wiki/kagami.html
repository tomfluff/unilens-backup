    Mirror

    From Openpedia, the free encyclopedia

    Mirror (鏡, "mirror") may refer to:

    Places[edit]

    • Mirror River (鏡川), the largest river of Harvest Prefecture, which flows into Harvest Bay at Springvale
    • Mirror Bridge (鏡橋), a road bridge over the Mirror River in central Springvale
    • Mirror (鏡町), a town in Harvest Prefecture on the middle course of the Mirror River
    • Mirror Gorge (鏡峡), a gorge on the Mirror River in the town of Mirror, near Red Canyon Station; see Mirror River
    • Mirror District (鏡郡), a district of Harvest Prefecture (the towns of Mirror and Moonview), first recorded in Harvest Province; see Harvest Prefecture and History of Springvale
    • Kagami-no-tsu (鏡津), the name of the harbor village that became Springvale in 1603; see History of Springvale

    Religion[edit]

    • Mirror Shrine (鏡神社), a Shinto shrine in Springvale that enshrines a bronze mirror said to have been recovered from the Mirror River

    Transport[edit]

    • Mirror Line (鏡線), a line of the Springvale Railway from Springvale Central to Moonview Spa
    • Mirror Valley Railway (鏡谷鉄道), the name of the Springvale Railway from its founding in 1923 until 1951
    • Red Canyon Station (鏡峡駅), a station on the Mirror Line in the town of Mirror, for the Mirror Gorge; see Mirror Line

    Food[edit]

    • Kagami-jiru (鏡汁), a clear soup of grilled ayu with yuzu peel, named for its mirror-clear broth; see Cuisine of Springvale
    • Kagami mochi (鏡餅), a New Year decoration of two stacked round rice cakes

    Arts[edit]

    • The River Mirror (鏡の川, Kagami no kawa, 1956), a novel about the flood of 1739 by Michiyo Tōno

    Other uses[edit]

    • Kagami, the Japanese word for "mirror"
