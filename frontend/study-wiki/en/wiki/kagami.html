    Kagami

    From Openpedia, the free encyclopedia

    Kagami (鏡, "mirror") may refer to:

    Places[edit]

    • Kagami River (鏡川), the largest river of Minori Prefecture, which flows into Minori Bay at Harukawa
    • Kagami Bridge (鏡橋), a road bridge over the Kagami River in central Harukawa
    • Kagami (鏡町), a town in Minori Prefecture on the middle course of the Kagami River
    • Kagami Gorge (鏡峡), a gorge on the Kagami River in the town of Kagami, near Kagami-kyō Station; see Kagami River
    • Kagami District (鏡郡), a district of Minori Prefecture (the towns of Kagami and Tsukimi), first recorded in Minori Province; see Minori Prefecture and History of Harukawa
    • Kagami-no-tsu (鏡津), the name of the harbor village that became Harukawa in 1603; see History of Harukawa

    Religion[edit]

    • Kagami Shrine (鏡神社), a Shinto shrine in Harukawa that enshrines a bronze mirror said to have been recovered from the Kagami River

    Transport[edit]

    • Kagami Line (鏡線), a line of the Harukawa Railway from Harukawa Central to Tsukimi Onsen
    • Kagami Valley Railway (鏡谷鉄道), the name of the Harukawa Railway from its founding in 1923 until 1951
    • Kagami-kyō Station (鏡峡駅), a station on the Kagami Line in the town of Kagami, for the Kagami Gorge; see Kagami Line

    Food[edit]

    • Kagami-jiru (鏡汁), a clear soup of grilled ayu with yuzu peel, named for its mirror-clear broth; see Cuisine of Harukawa
    • Kagami mochi (鏡餅), a New Year decoration of two stacked round rice cakes

    Arts[edit]

    • The River Mirror (鏡の川, Kagami no kawa, 1956), a novel about the flood of 1739 by Michiyo Tōno

    Other uses[edit]

    • Kagami, the Japanese word for "mirror"
