    RideCard (ハルカ, Haruka) is a rechargeable contactless IC card issued by the Springvale Railway (Vale Rail) in Springvale, Harvest Prefecture. It was launched on 1 March 2012 on both the Mirror Line and the Bayside Line. It is also accepted on the municipal Springvale City Bus and on Moonview Bus in the upper valley, but not on the airport limousine bus to Harvest Airport, and it can be used as electronic money in shops in the stations, including Springvale Central Terrace. About 410,000 cards were in circulation in 2024.[1]

    A card costs a refundable deposit of ¥500 and can hold up to ¥20,000. Fares paid with RideCard are up to ¥18 cheaper than paper tickets: Springvale Central to Moonview Spa, for example, costs ¥715 instead of ¥730. Child cards are charged half fare, and commuter passes can be loaded onto the card. Cards are sold from ticket machines and at the RideCard service counter of Springvale Central Station. RideCard pays the ordinary fare only; the limited express Comet also needs a reserved-seat ticket. A phone version, Mobile RideCard, was introduced in 2024 and can also hold ticketless Comet reservations.[2]

    The name combines Haru (from Springvale) and "Card", and is also read as the word haruka (遥か, "far away").
