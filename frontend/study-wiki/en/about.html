    About Openpedia

    From Openpedia, the free encyclopedia

    Openpedia is a free online encyclopedia written collaboratively by its readers. This copy is a fictional website made for a research study of UniLens, an assistant for people with low vision. Its articles describe the fictional city of Harukawa and Minori Prefecture; the people, places and organisations in them do not exist.

    Help

    Use the search box at the top of every page to find an article. Suggestions appear as you type; press Enter to see all results. Every article links to related articles, and the categories at the bottom of each article list similar pages. The language link at the top right opens the same page in Japanese.

    Citations and "citation needed"

    Openpedia articles cite their sources in numbered footnotes. A statement marked [citation needed] has not yet been supported by a reliable source. To cite an article, give its title, "Openpedia" and the date you read it.
