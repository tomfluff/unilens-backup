Castle Town Station

しろまち · 城町
Mirror Line: ‹ Springvale Central Castle Town University Gate ›
TimetablesPlatformsFacilitiesStation planExits & busesNearbyFares

The station for Springvale Castle and the old castle town. On the Lantern Festival evenings it is the busiest station on the line and entry is restricted.

Address: 3-2 Castle Gate, Springvale
