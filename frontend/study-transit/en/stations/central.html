Springvale Central Station

はるかわちゅうおう · 春川中央
Mirror Line: Springvale Central Castle Town ›
Bayside Line: Springvale Central Bridge Street ›
TimetablesPlatformsFacilitiesStation planExits & busesNearbyFares

The hub of the Vale Rail network, where the Mirror Line and the Bayside Line meet. All Comet limited expresses start and end here. The North Exit faces the bus terminal and the castle district; the South Exit faces the bay and the Bayside Line concourse.

Address: 1-1 Station Square, Springvale
