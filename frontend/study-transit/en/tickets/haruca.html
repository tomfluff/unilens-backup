HaruCa IC card

HaruCa (ハルカ) is Harukawa Railway's rechargeable IC card. Touch it on the reader at the ticket gate when you enter and when you leave; the fare is deducted automatically and is up to ¥18 cheaper than a paper ticket.

Types of HaruCa

CardFeaturesWhere to get it
HaruCaAnyone can use it. Not replaced if lost.Ticket machines at staffed stations
Personalized HaruCaRegistered in your name. Reissued if lost. Needed for HaruCa Points and ticketless Tsukikage.Ticket machines (Harukawa Central, Shiromachi, Kamano, Minori Port) and ticket offices
Child HaruCaCharges child fares until 31 March after the child's 12th birthday.Ticket offices only; bring proof of age
HaruCa CommuterA commuter pass on a Personalized HaruCa.Ticket offices; renew at machines
HaruCa AssistFor holders of a disability certificate; charges discounted fares.Ticket offices at Harukawa Central and Minori Port
Silver HaruCaAged 65 or over: 10% off off-peak fares (annual fee ¥1,000).Ticket offices; bring ID

Buying a HaruCa

Cards are sold for ¥2,000, ¥3,000, ¥5,000 or ¥10,000. Each price includes a refundable deposit of ¥500; the rest is stored value (for example, a ¥2,000 card has ¥1,500 to spend).

Charging (topping up)

  • Charge ¥1,000, ¥2,000, ¥3,000, ¥5,000 or ¥10,000 at ticket machines and charge machines. Cash only.
  • The maximum balance is ¥20,000.
  • Charging is not possible at unstaffed stations (Ōtsuka, Nakazawa, Hotarudani, Shiohama, Hamanaka) or on trains.
  • With HaruCa Web you can charge a Personalized HaruCa online by credit card (from ¥1,000); the charge is added the next time you touch a ticket gate, at least 30 minutes later.

Using HaruCa

  • You need a balance of at least ¥170 to enter. If your balance does not cover the fare when you leave, pay the difference at the fare adjustment machine inside the gate.
  • Exit within 3 hours of entering. Otherwise the gate stops you and staff will charge the fare from your entry station.
  • HaruCa can be used on Harukawa City Bus and Tsukimi Bus, and in shops inside our stations. It cannot be used on the airport limousine bus.
  • IC cards issued by other railways in Japan cannot be used on Harukawa Railway, and HaruCa cannot be used outside Minori Prefecture.
  • The Tsukikage surcharge can be paid from your HaruCa balance at reserved-seat ticket machines. If you register a Personalized HaruCa when you reserve online, you can board without a paper ticket.

HaruCa Points

With a Personalized HaruCa registered on HaruCa Web, you earn points when you ride the same section (the same two stations, in either direction) many times in a calendar month:

Rides on the same section in the monthPoints
1st to 10thNone
11th to 20th10% of the fare
21st and later15% of the fare

1 point = ¥1. Points are added in the middle of the following month and are turned into balance when you touch "Points" on a ticket machine. Sections covered by a commuter pass, 1-Day passes and Tsukikage surcharges do not earn points. Points expire after 12 months.

Refunds

Return your HaruCa at a ticket office: Harukawa Central, Shiromachi, Harukawa Daigaku-mae (weekdays only), Kamano, Kagami-kyō, Tsukimi Onsen, Minori Port or Minori-kōen (Saturdays, Sundays and holidays only). We refund the balance minus a fee of ¥220, plus the ¥500 deposit. If the balance is ¥220 or less, the balance is not refunded but the deposit is. A card with a commuter pass on it is refunded under the commuter pass rules.

If you lose a HaruCa

A Personalized HaruCa, Child HaruCa, HaruCa Commuter or HaruCa Assist can be stopped and reissued. Report the loss at a ticket office with ID; the balance is protected from that moment. The new card is ready from 10:00 the next day at the same office, for a reissue fee of ¥520 and a new ¥500 deposit. An unregistered HaruCa cannot be reissued.

A HaruCa that has not been used for 10 years becomes invalid.
