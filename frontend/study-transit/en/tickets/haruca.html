RideCard IC card

RideCard (ハルカ) is Springvale Railway's rechargeable IC card. Touch it on the reader at the ticket gate when you enter and when you leave; the fare is deducted automatically and is up to ¥18 cheaper than a paper ticket.

Types of RideCard

CardFeaturesWhere to get it
RideCardAnyone can use it. Not replaced if lost.Ticket machines at staffed stations
Personalized RideCardRegistered in your name. Reissued if lost. Needed for RideCard Points and ticketless Comet.Ticket machines (Springvale Central, Castle Town, Clayfield, Ferry Port) and ticket offices
Child RideCardCharges child fares until 31 March after the child's 12th birthday.Ticket offices only; bring proof of age
RideCard CommuterA commuter pass on a Personalized RideCard.Ticket offices; renew at machines
RideCard AssistFor holders of a disability certificate; charges discounted fares.Ticket offices at Springvale Central and Ferry Port
Silver RideCardAged 65 or over: 10% off off-peak fares (annual fee ¥1,000).Ticket offices; bring ID

Buying a RideCard

Cards are sold for ¥2,000, ¥3,000, ¥5,000 or ¥10,000. Each price includes a refundable deposit of ¥500; the rest is stored value (for example, a ¥2,000 card has ¥1,500 to spend).

Charging (topping up)

  • Charge ¥1,000, ¥2,000, ¥3,000, ¥5,000 or ¥10,000 at ticket machines and charge machines. Cash only.
  • The maximum balance is ¥20,000.
  • Charging is not possible at unstaffed stations (Hilltop, Mill Creek, Firefly Valley, Tidewater, Lighthouse) or on trains.
  • With RideCard Web you can charge a Personalized RideCard online by credit card (from ¥1,000); the charge is added the next time you touch a ticket gate, at least 30 minutes later.

Using RideCard

  • You need a balance of at least ¥170 to enter. If your balance does not cover the fare when you leave, pay the difference at the fare adjustment machine inside the gate.
  • Exit within 3 hours of entering. Otherwise the gate stops you and staff will charge the fare from your entry station.
  • RideCard can be used on Springvale City Bus and Moonview Bus, and in shops inside our stations. It cannot be used on the airport limousine bus.
  • IC cards issued by other railways in Japan cannot be used on Springvale Railway, and RideCard cannot be used outside Harvest Prefecture.
  • The Comet surcharge can be paid from your RideCard balance at reserved-seat ticket machines. If you register a Personalized RideCard when you reserve online, you can board without a paper ticket.

RideCard Points

With a Personalized RideCard registered on RideCard Web, you earn points when you ride the same section (the same two stations, in either direction) many times in a calendar month:

Rides on the same section in the monthPoints
1st to 10thNone
11th to 20th10% of the fare
21st and later15% of the fare

1 point = ¥1. Points are added in the middle of the following month and are turned into balance when you touch "Points" on a ticket machine. Sections covered by a commuter pass, 1-Day passes and Comet surcharges do not earn points. Points expire after 12 months.

Refunds

Return your RideCard at a ticket office: Springvale Central, Castle Town, University Gate (weekdays only), Clayfield, Red Canyon, Moonview Spa, Ferry Port or Rose Garden (Saturdays, Sundays and holidays only). We refund the balance minus a fee of ¥220, plus the ¥500 deposit. If the balance is ¥220 or less, the balance is not refunded but the deposit is. A card with a commuter pass on it is refunded under the commuter pass rules.

If you lose a RideCard

A Personalized RideCard, Child RideCard, RideCard Commuter or RideCard Assist can be stopped and reissued. Report the loss at a ticket office with ID; the balance is protected from that moment. The new card is ready from 10:00 the next day at the same office, for a reissue fee of ¥520 and a new ¥500 deposit. An unregistered RideCard cannot be reissued.

A RideCard that has not been used for 10 years becomes invalid.
