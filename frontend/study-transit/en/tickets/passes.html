Passes and package tickets

1-Day Free Pass

PriceWeekdays: adult ¥1,300, child ¥650
Saturdays, Sundays and holidays: adult ¥1,100, child ¥550
ValidUnlimited travel on Local and Rapid trains on both lines on one day. On Tsukikage, also buy a limited express surcharge ticket.
WhereTicket machines at staffed stations (valid on the day of purchase), or ticket offices up to one month ahead (choose the date). Can also be loaded onto HaruCa at a ticket machine ("HaruCa 1-Day"); the pass then runs until the last train that day.
Extras¥100 off at Tsukimi Sōyu public bath; 10% off pottery workshops at Harukawa Ware Hall (Kamano).
RefundUnused passes only, before the day of validity, with a ¥220 fee.

Is it worth it? A return trip from Harukawa Central to Tsukimi Onsen costs ¥1,460 with paper tickets or ¥1,430 with HaruCa, so the pass pays for itself on that trip on any day. For shorter trips, such as Harukawa Central to Kamano (¥324 each way with HaruCa), it pays only if you make several journeys.

Rail & Bus 1-Day Pass

¥1,700 (child ¥850). Everything in the 1-Day Free Pass, plus Harukawa City Bus routes on the same day (not Tsukimi Bus or the airport limousine). Sold at ticket offices and at the City Bus centre at Harukawa Central North Exit. Paper ticket only.

Tsukimi Onsen Yukemuri Ticket

PriceAdult ¥2,800, child ¥1,400 (from any Harukawa Railway station)
IncludesA return journey between your station and Tsukimi Onsen (Local and Rapid); a Tsukikage standard reserved seat for one journey each way (reserve your seats when you buy the ticket, or later at a ticket office or reserved-seat machine, free); one entry to Tsukimi Sōyu or Kogane-yu public bath; a ¥500 voucher for shops and restaurants in Tsukimi Onsen.
Valid2 consecutive days. You may break your journey at Kamano and Kagami-kyō only.
Not valid10–12 October 2026 (Lantern Festival) and 28 December 2026 – 6 January 2027.
WhereTicket offices at Harukawa Central, Shiromachi, Kamano and Minori Port, by 21:00 the day before you travel. Not sold at Tsukimi Onsen, at ticket machines or online.
Premium seatsUpgrade for ¥700 per journey.
RefundBefore the first day of validity, with a ¥220 fee. Not refundable once used.
Sales periodUntil 31 March 2027 (extended).
