Bayside Line

The Bayside Line runs 16.2 km east from Springvale Central along Harvest Bay, past the fish market at Market Square and Rose Garden, to Ferry Port and its ferry terminal. It is double track and elevated between Springvale Central and Pier Road. All trains are locals stopping at every station; the last train of the day terminates at Rose Garden.

Frequency

  • Weekdays: every 15 minutes in the morning and evening peaks (6:45–9:00, 17:00–19:45), every 20 minutes during the day.
  • Saturdays, Sundays and holidays: every 20 minutes from 6:00 to 21:00, then every 30 minutes.
