Kagami Line

The Kagami Line runs 38.6 km north from Harukawa Central up the Kagami River valley, through the pottery village of Kamano and the Kagami Gorge, to the hot-spring town of Tsukimi Onsen. It is single track north of Kamano, with passing loops at every station except Hotarudani. Local, Rapid and Ltd. Exp. Tsukikage trains run on the line; many locals start or end at Kamano.

Frequency

  • Weekdays: 2–3 locals an hour as far as Kamano, one an hour beyond (two in the evening peak); one Rapid an hour 7:00–20:00; Tsukikage every 2 hours.
  • Saturdays, Sundays and holidays: one local an hour to Tsukimi Onsen plus one to Kamano 9:45–18:45; one Rapid an hour 8:00–18:00; Tsukikage hourly in the morning, every 2 hours from 11:30.
