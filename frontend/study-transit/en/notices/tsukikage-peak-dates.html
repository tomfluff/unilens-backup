Tsukikage peak dates for 2026–27

25 Aug 2026 Tickets

On the following peak dates, the Tsukikage limited express surcharge is ¥200 higher than usual (for both standard and Premium seats; children ¥100):

10–12 Oct 2026, 28 Dec 2026–6 Jan 2027, 27 Apr–6 May 2027, 8–17 Aug 2027

The Tsukimi Onsen Yukemuri Ticket cannot be used from 10 to 12 October 2026 or from 28 December 2026 to 6 January 2027.
