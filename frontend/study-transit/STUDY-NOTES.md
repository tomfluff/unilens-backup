### 1. Last train back on a Sunday ✓
- **EN:** "I'm going to Moonview Spa this Sunday. What is the last train I can take back to Springvale Central?"
- **JA:** 「今度の日曜日に月見温泉へ行きます。春川中央に帰る最終列車は何時ですか。」
- **Where:** Moonview Spa timetable, southbound (Saturday/holiday tab, "First and last trains"); Moonview Spa trip page; FAQ; timetable revision notice.
- **Why interesting:** weekday and weekend tabs differ by 35 minutes; the last Comet is much earlier; the answer changes from 14 November (in a notice, not in the timetable).
- **Answer:** 21:05 Local, arriving Springvale Central 22:08. The last Comet is 19:50 (No. 28, arr. 20:29). From Sunday 15 November the last train is 21:40 (arr. 22:43). (Weekdays: 21:40.)

### 2. Cheapest way to make three return trips in a week
- **EN:** "Next week I'll go from Springvale Central to Moonview Spa and back on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday. I don't need the limited express. What's the cheapest way to pay?"
- **JA:** 「来週の月・水・土曜日に、春川中央と月見温泉を往復します。特急は使いません。いちばん安い払い方は？」
- **Where:** Passes (1-Day Free Pass, weekday and weekend prices), fares (RideCard vs ticket), RideCard Points, commuter passes.
- **Why interesting:** needs comparing four options across pages and noticing the weekend price is lower.
- **Answer:** A 1-Day Free Pass each day: ¥1,300 + ¥1,300 + ¥1,100 = **¥3,700**. RideCard would be ¥715 × 6 = ¥4,290; paper tickets ¥730 × 6 = ¥4,380; RideCard Points start only from the 11th ride on a section in a month; a 1-month commuter pass (36–40 km) is ¥20,810.

### 3. Northbound platforms without step-free access ✓
- **EN:** "I use a wheelchair. At which stations can't I reach the platform for trains towards Moonview Spa without stairs?"
- **JA:** 「車いすを使っています。月見温泉方面のホームに階段を使わずに行けない駅はどこですか。」
- **Where:** Accessibility page (step-free table), station pages (platform tables), line page, Castle Town lift notice, status page.
- **Why interesting:** spread over 22 station pages or one dense table; a temporary exception in a notice; "no lift" is not the same as "no step-free route" (ramps, level access).
- **Answer:** West Meadow (K06), Citrus Grove (K09) and Mill Creek (K11): platform 1 is stairs only. Also Castle Town (K02): the platform 1 lift is out of service until 23 October (staff route 7:00–20:00). Hilltop and Trailhead have ramps; Firefly Valley and Moonview Spa have level access (no lift needed). On the Bayside Line, Tidewater's eastbound platform is stairs only.

### 4. Getting off at Castle Town in a wheelchair in the evening
- **EN:** "On 15 October at about 21:00 I'm taking a train from Springvale Central to Castle Town in my wheelchair. Can I get off there?"
- **JA:** 「10月15日の21時ごろ、車いすで春川中央から城町まで電車に乗ります。城町で降りられますか。」
- **Where:** Castle Town lift notice; Castle Town station page; accessibility page.
- **Why interesting:** a multi-step workaround in fine print, with a time window that excludes 21:00.
- **Answer:** Northbound trains arrive at platform 1, whose lift is out of service until 23 October. The staff route works only 7:00–20:00, so ride on to University Gate (lifts on both platforms), change to a southbound train and come back to Castle Town platform 2 (lift working). No extra fare; tell the gate staff.

### 5. Two seats with a river view
- **EN:** "Book two seats together on a morning Comet to Moonview Spa on Saturday 17 October, on the side with the river view."
- **JA:** 「10月17日（土）午前の「月影」で、月見温泉まで川が見える側の席を2人並びで予約してください。」
- **Where:** Comet page (seats: "D side northbound"), reservation flow and SVG seat map.
- **Why interesting:** the rule is in the small print; the seat map is a clickable SVG with taken seats.
- **Answer:** Northbound the river is on the D side (between Clayfield and Red Canyon), so choose a C+D pair in Car 2 or 3 (Car 1 Premium has only one D seat per row). Morning trains: 7:30 (No. 1), 8:30 (3), 9:30 (5), 10:30 (7), 11:30 (9). Not a peak date: ¥1,490 each (¥730 fare + ¥760 surcharge), ¥2,980 for two.

### 6. Cancelling a Premium seat the day before ✓
- **EN:** "I bought a Premium Comet ticket from Springvale Central to Moonview Spa at the ticket office for tomorrow, but I can't go. How much do I get back?"
- **JA:** 「明日の「月影」プレミアム席（春川中央〜月見温泉）をきっぷうりばで買いましたが、行けなくなりました。いくら戻りますか。」
- **Where:** Comet page (changes and refunds table, examples), rules (fare ticket refund).
- **Why interesting:** a percentage with a minimum, the Premium fee included in the base, and the fare ticket refunded separately.
- **Answer:** Surcharge + Premium = ¥760 + ¥700 = ¥1,460; fee 30% = ¥438 (above the ¥340 minimum), so ¥1,022 back; the unused fare ticket ¥730 − ¥220 = ¥510. Total **¥1,532**. (Alternatively, one free change to another Comet before departure.)

### 7. Arriving by 11:00 from Ferry Port
- **EN:** "On Saturday 17 October I need to be at Moonview Spa by 11:00, coming from Ferry Port. When should I leave and what will it cost?"
- **JA:** 「10月17日（土）、みのり港から月見温泉に11時までに着きたいです。何時に出ればよく、いくらかかりますか。」
- **Where:** Journey planner (arrive by), Bayside and Mirror timetables, fares.
- **Why interesting:** a change at Springvale Central between two lines; cost differs a lot with and without Comet.
- **Answer:** Latest: 9:10 from Ferry Port, change at Springvale Central to the 10:00 Rapid, arrive 10:55, ¥970 (RideCard ¥952). Faster: 8:50, change to Comet 5 at 9:30, arrive 10:09, ¥1,730.

### 8. Home to Ferry Port after the Lantern Festival
- **EN:** "On Saturday 10 October I want to watch the Lantern Festival until it ends, then go home to Ferry Port. What's my last train, and which station should I use?"
- **JA:** 「10月10日（土）、灯籠まつりを最後まで見てからみのり港に帰りたいです。最終は何時で、どの駅を使えばいいですか。」
- **Where:** Lantern Festival notice (extra trains table), festival trip page, Bayside timetable.
- **Why interesting:** the extra trains are only in a notice; the planner and timetables say they are not included.
- **Answer:** The festival ends at 21:00. Avoid Castle Town (entry restricted 18:00–21:30); walk to Springvale Central North Exit. Regular last Bayside train to Ferry Port 23:00 (arr. 23:28); extra trains at 21:15, 21:45, 22:15, 22:45 and 23:30 (arr. 23:58), the last one. Buy the ticket or charge RideCard beforehand.

### 9. Fare for an adult and a child ✓
- **EN:** "How much is it for me and my 7-year-old from Ferry Port to Moonview Spa with RideCard? And if we take Comet?"
- **JA:** 「みのり港から月見温泉まで、私と7歳の子どもでハルカを使うといくらですか。「月影」に乗る場合は？」
- **Where:** Fares (child rounding), fare matrix or calculator, Comet surcharges (child half).
- **Why interesting:** child rounding rules differ for RideCard and tickets; the surcharge applies only to the Mirror Line part.
- **Answer:** RideCard ¥952 + ¥476 = **¥1,428** one way. With Comet (standard): + ¥760 + ¥380 = **¥2,568**.

### 10. Bicycle to Moonview Spa
- **EN:** "Can I take my bicycle (not folding) from Clayfield to Moonview Spa on Sunday morning? Which train?"
- **JA:** 「日曜の午前中、折りたたみでない自転車を釜野から月見温泉まで持ち込めますか。どの列車ですか。」
- **Where:** Rules (bicycles, Cycle Train), Clayfield northbound timetable (weekend tab).
- **Why interesting:** a rule with four conditions plus a timetable lookup.
- **Answer:** Yes, on the Cycle Train: weekends and holidays, Local trains leaving 10:00–16:00, Clayfield–Moonview Spa only, ¥300 per bicycle, up to 4 per train, front car (not 10–12 October). The 9:38 is too early; the first is the **10:38** Local (then 11:38, …).

### 13. Student commuter pass
- **EN:** "My daughter commutes from Market Square to Springvale University. How much is a 3-month student pass, and how do we buy it?"
- **JA:** 「娘が市場から春川大学に通っています。3か月の通学定期券はいくらで、どうやって買いますか。」
- **Where:** Commuter pass table, line pages or fare matrix for distance, station page (University Gate), discounts.
- **Why interesting:** distance via Springvale Central, a banded table, and conditions.
- **Answer:** Market Square (3.2 km) + University Gate (3.6 km) = 6.8 km → 7–10 km band: **¥11,630** for 3 months (1 month ¥4,080, 6 months ¥22,030). New passes only at a ticket office, with a commuting certificate from the school.

### 14. Umbrella left on a train
- **EN:** "I left my umbrella on a Comet on Thursday 1 October. Is it still kept, and where can I collect it?"
- **JA:** 「10月1日（木）に「月影」に傘を忘れました。まだ保管されていますか。どこで受け取れますか。」
- **Where:** Lost and found (retention table, centre hours).
- **Why interesting:** different retention periods by item type.
- **Answer:** Umbrellas are kept 14 days (until about 14 October) at the Lost Property Centre, Springvale Central South Exit next to the ticket office, 9:00–18:00 (050-3000-8355). Bring photo ID; or use the online form.

### 15. Delay certificate ✓
- **EN:** "My train on Thursday 1 October was late because of the rain and I was late for work. Can I still get a delay certificate?"
- **JA:** 「10月1日（木）、雨で電車が遅れて会社に遅刻しました。今から遅延証明書をもらえますか。」
- **Where:** Service status (history, delay certificates), rules (section 4).
- **Why interesting:** a 7-day window, and stations issue on the day only.
- **Answer:** Yes, until 7 October: print it from the service status page (Mirror Line, heavy rain from about 6:00, delays up to 18 minutes; Comet 2 and 6 cancelled between Moonview Spa and Red Canyon). Stations no longer issue it.
