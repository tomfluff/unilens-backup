Kagami Kiln

Harukawa ware from a family kiln in Kamagaoka, since 1891

4.8 (612 ratings) · 98% positive in the last 12 months

Kamagaoka pottery district, Harukawa, Minori Prefecture · Selling on Marketa since 2020

Kagami Kiln was founded in 1891 in Kamagaoka, the hillside pottery district above the Kagami River where Harukawa ware has been made since the castle town was young. Today the kiln is run by the fourth-generation potter Kagami Sōichi, with his wife Yoshiko on glazes and their daughter Mio on the wheel.

Every piece is thrown on the wheel from clay dug in the Kagami River valley and fired in our gas kiln; twice a year we also fire the old climbing kiln with wood. Our best-known glazes are the yuzu-yellow ash glaze, made with ash from pruned yuzu branches from a farm up the valley, a deep indigo, celadon crackle, amber, iron brown and white ash with blue drips.

On Marketa: the donabe rice pot, the mug pair, the kyusu tea set, the plate set of 5 and the sake set.

About handmade ware: no two pieces are the same. Glaze, colour and size vary from the photos, and small pinholes in the glaze are part of the nature of handmade pottery, not a defect. Our donabe is for gas flames only (not IH, microwave or dishwasher) and must be seasoned with rice porridge before first use; the iron-brown plate is not for the microwave.
