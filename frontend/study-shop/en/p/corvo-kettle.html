Product description

The Corvo Quick-Boil is a plain, compact kettle for people who mostly boil water for one or two cups at a time: tea, instant coffee, cup noodles, a baby bottle. It holds 1.0 L, enough for about five mugs, and its 1,250 W element is the most powerful a standard 100 V wall socket supports comfortably.

Inside, the water touches stainless steel only, with no plastic water gauge. The trade-off is that you cannot see the water level from outside: the fill marks are inside, so you look in through the lid. The lid opens with a button on the handle and stops upright at 75°.

The body is a single layer of steel, so the outside gets hot when the water boils and stays warm for several minutes. Keep it out of reach of small children. Wipe the outside with a soft cloth; to remove limescale, boil 0.8 L of water with 1 tablespoon of citric acid, leave for an hour and rinse.

Corvo covers the kettle and base for one year from purchase. Keep your Marketa order confirmation as proof of purchase; you do not need to register the product.

No. Inside it is stainless steel with a silicone seal on the lid. The inner lid is polypropylene but it only touches steam, not the water, unless you tip the kettle over.

Answer:
No. It is made for 100 V only. Using it on 220–240 V, even with a plug adapter, will damage it and is not covered by the warranty.
