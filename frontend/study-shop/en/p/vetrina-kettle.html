Product description

The Vetrina is the largest kettle in this range: 1.7 L, enough for a family's morning tea and a jug of barley tea in one go. The body is borosilicate glass, the heat-resistant glass used for coffee servers and laboratory flasks, so you can see exactly how much water is inside and watch it come to the boil under a blue LED.

It does one thing: it boils. There are no temperature settings and no keep-warm, and because it holds so much, a full kettle takes about 6 minutes, nearly twice as long as a 1.0 L kettle. Fill only what you need for faster boiling. Do not fill above the 1.7 L mark: boiling water can be pushed out past the lid seal, and when pouring a full kettle make sure the lid is pressed down until it clicks at the front and the back.

The tea infuser version comes with a stainless mesh basket that hangs from the rim. Boil the water first, open the lid, lower the basket in and close the lid to steep. Never switch the kettle on with the basket or tea leaves inside: tannin stains the glass and clogs the spout filter. Glass also shows limescale as a white film, especially with hard water; boil 1.0 L of water with 2 tablespoons of citric acid, leave it for an hour and rinse.

This item is sold and shipped by BrightDeal Trading from its warehouse in Kobe. Shipping is free and it arrives 4–8 days after ordering; it is not covered by Marketa Fast, so it cannot come next day.
