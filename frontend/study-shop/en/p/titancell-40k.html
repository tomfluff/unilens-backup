Product description

The TitanCell 40000 is a large-capacity power bank for times when you are far from a socket: camping, festivals, long trips and power cuts after typhoons or earthquakes. Keep it charged in your emergency kit and you can keep a family's phones running for several days.

Its maximum output is 22.5 W, which suits phones, tablets, earbuds, LED lanterns and USB fans. It cannot charge laptops. Each port gives its full speed only when used alone; with two or three devices connected, they share 15 W.

Recharging a 148 Wh battery takes time: about 11 hours with a USB-C charger of 18 W or more, or about 18 hours through micro-USB. In storage a full charge drops by about 3% a month, so top it up every three months.

Flying: at 148 Wh it is between 100 and 160 Wh. You must get approval from your airline before you fly, you may carry at most two such batteries, and it can never go in checked baggage. To fly without approval, choose a power bank under 100 Wh.
