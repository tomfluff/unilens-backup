Product description

A rich hand cream made with oil pressed from the seeds of the yuzu we grow above the Mirror River, together with shea butter, squalane and rice bran oil. It is made for hands that dry out and crack in winter, from washing up, frequent hand washing or cold wind.

Scent: the only scent is yuzu peel oil from our own fruit. It smells fresh and slightly bitter, like the peel itself rather than sweets, and fades to a faint trace in 20–30 minutes. It varies a little from one harvest to the next. If you want a scent that lasts for hours, this is not that kind of cream.

How to use: squeeze out a pea-sized amount (about 0.5 g) and rub it in well, including between the fingers and over the knuckles. It feels slightly oily for the first few minutes, so during the day use a little less and wait a moment before touching your phone or paper. At night, a thicker layer under cotton gloves works well for cracked knuckles.

Skin: citrus peel oil can sting cuts and open cracks and may cause redness on sensitive skin. Before the first use, patch test on the inside of your arm and wait 24 hours, and do not apply it to broken skin. If you have eczema, ask a dermatologist first.

Sizes and returns: both tubes contain the same cream. The 40 g fits a coat pocket; the 80 g is better value and lasts about three months with daily use. Use within 6 months of opening. Sold by Harvest Yuzu Farm and shipped by Marketa: unopened tubes can be returned within 30 days; opened cosmetics only if defective or damaged.
