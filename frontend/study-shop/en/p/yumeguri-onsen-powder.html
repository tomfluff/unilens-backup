Product description

Tsukimi Onsen, at the end of the HaruTetsu Kagami Line, is known for its soft, slightly slippery water, a sodium bicarbonate spring often called a "bath for beautiful skin". Yumeguri's bath powder recreates its main components at home with sodium bicarbonate and sodium sulfate. It does not contain any spring water from Tsukimi Onsen.

The water turns an opaque milky green, with a forest scent of hinoki and green leaves. Both are added for atmosphere; the real spring is clear and almost odourless. As a medicated bath powder (quasi-drug), its effects on fatigue, stiff shoulders, lower-back pain, feeling cold and rough skin are approved by Japan's health ministry; the full list is in the specifications.

How to use: tear open one packet, add it to 150–200 L of water at 38–41 °C and stir. Soak for 10–15 minutes. The tear notch is small; with wet hands, scissors are easier. Rinse the tub soon after draining, as the milky ingredients can leave a faint white film.

Bath equipment: it contains no sulfur, so you can reheat (追い焚き) with one-hole and two-hole systems. Do not use it in 24-hour circulating baths or jetted baths, or in wooden or natural-marble tubs, which it can stain. Leftover water can be used for the laundry wash cycle, but rinse with fresh water and do not use it for wool, silk or white clothes.

Cautions: stop using it if your skin becomes red or itchy, and consult a doctor. Babies and small children should bathe only with an adult; for babies under 1 year, ask your doctor first. Do not drink it. Unopened boxes can be returned within 30 days of delivery; opened boxes only if defective or damaged.
