Product description

The Komekko IH is a standard family rice cooker: big enough for a family of four, small enough for a narrow counter, and simple to use. Choose the menu, press Start, and in about 48 minutes you have rice that stays warm and soft until dinner.

Rice cooker sizes in Japan are given in cups (gō). One cup is the 180 mL measure in the box, about 150 g of uncooked rice, and makes about two rice bowls. "5.5 cups (1.0 L)" means it cooks up to 5.5 of these cups, about 990 mL of uncooked rice. It cooks well from 0.5 cup, so one person can use it too, although a 3-cup cooker such as the Aoba Mini takes less space and costs less.

Induction heating warms the whole pot rather than only the bottom, which gives more even, firmer rice than a microcomputer cooker. It is not a pressure cooker: if you want stickier, sweeter rice and softer brown rice, the Inaho Pressure IH cooks under pressure but costs more than twice as much. Steam comes straight up from the vent at the back, so leave about 30 cm of space above the cooker and do not cook under a low cupboard.

After each use, wash the inner pot, the removable inner lid and the steam cap. Use the paddle provided and a soft sponge: metal utensils, scouring pads and the dishwasher damage the coating. The coating is a consumable part and is not covered by the warranty if it wears or peels; a replacement pot is available from Komekko customer support.

The display is a plain LCD without a backlight, so it can be hard to read in dim light; the Start button is the largest and has a raised dot. Komekko covers the cooker for one year from purchase. Keep your Marketa order confirmation as proof of purchase.
