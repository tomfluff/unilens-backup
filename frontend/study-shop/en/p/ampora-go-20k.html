Product description

The Ampora Go 20K is a power bank for people who carry a laptop as well as a phone. Its main USB-C port delivers 65 W, the same as the charger that comes with most 13–14-inch laptops, so it charges them at normal speed even while you work.

Which laptops? Any laptop that charges over USB-C Power Delivery. If your laptop's own charger is 65 W or less, the Go 20K charges it at full speed. Laptops that come with 90–140 W chargers, such as 15–16-inch performance and creator laptops, charge slowly when idle and may still lose charge under heavy load; for those, the Kestrel 27K (140 W) is the better choice. Laptops with a round barrel connector cannot be charged.

When you connect or disconnect a second device, all ports pause for about a second while the power bank shares out the power again. A laptop simply runs on its own battery for that second. In a bag, the power bank gets warm when it delivers 65 W for a long time; keep it in an outside pocket, not wrapped in clothes.

Capacity is 74 Wh. After conversion losses about 55 Wh reaches a laptop: one full charge of a 13-inch laptop with a 50 Wh battery, or about four charges of a typical phone. Ampora covers it for 18 months, and a swollen battery is always replaced free.
