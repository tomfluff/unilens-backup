Product description

Nami Buds 2 are everyday noise-cancelling earbuds for commuting, working and calls. Noise cancelling has three levels: High for trains and planes, Medium for offices and cafés, and Low for windy streets, where strong cancelling can cause a whooshing sound. Ambient mode lets outside sound in so you can hear announcements or talk at a till without taking a bud out.

They work with any Bluetooth phone, tablet or computer. iPhones use AAC; most Android phones use AAC or SBC. There is no LDAC or aptX, so Android users who want high-resolution Bluetooth audio should look at over-ear headphones such as the Seiran Studio NC. Multipoint is on by default: the buds stay connected to two devices, for example your phone and a laptop, and switch to whichever one plays sound or rings. In very crowded places such as station platforms at rush hour, turning multipoint off in the app can make the connection steadier.

The buds come with the medium tips fitted. Most people get a better seal, and much better noise cancelling, with the right size, so try the fit test in the app. Very small ears may still find them loose when running. Each bud has three microphones; calls are clear indoors and on quiet streets, but in strong wind or on a bicycle callers will hear you less well.

Care: wipe the ear tips and the gold charging contacts with a dry cloth once a week; earwax on the contacts is the most common reason one bud stops charging. Nami covers the earbuds and case for one year from purchase. A single lost earbud can be replaced through Nami support for ¥4,400 (case ¥5,500).

Can I connect them to my iPhone and my Windows laptop at the same time?
Answer:
Yes. Multipoint keeps two devices connected at once. Pair each device once, and the earbuds reconnect to both when you take them out of the case. Sound comes from whichever device is playing, and a call on either device takes priority.
