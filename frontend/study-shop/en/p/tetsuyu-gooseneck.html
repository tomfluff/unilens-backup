Product description

The Tetsuyu is made for pour-over coffee and Japanese tea, where both the water temperature and the speed of the pour change the taste. Set any temperature from 40 to 100 °C on the base, and the long gooseneck spout lets you pour a stream as thin as a pencil or as full as you like, without splashing the grounds.

It is not a family kettle. It holds 0.8 L, but the comfortable pouring amount is 0.6 L, enough for two or three mugs of drip coffee. The 1,000 W heater is gentler than the 1,200–1,250 W of the other kettles here, so 0.6 L takes just over 3 minutes. If you mostly need a lot of hot water quickly, for cooking, cup noodles or a family pot of tea, a 1.2–1.7 L kettle will suit you better.

To use: fill, place on the base, press the power button, set the temperature with + and – (hold to change 5 °C at a time) and press START. It beeps three times when it reaches the temperature. Press HOLD to keep it there for up to 60 minutes. To silence the beeps, hold HOLD and – together for 3 seconds. The base remembers your last temperature. The buttons are flat touch keys without raised marks.

The outside is single-wall steel with a matte powder coating. It gets hot while heating, and the coating can mark if you scrub it: wipe it with a soft damp cloth, never an abrasive sponge. Matte White shows coffee splashes more readily; Matte Black shows white limescale drips. Descale the inside monthly: 1 teaspoon of citric acid in 0.5 L of water, heat to 100 °C, leave for an hour and rinse.

Tetsuyu covers the kettle and base for two years from purchase, including the display. Wear to the outer coating is not covered. Keep your Marketa order confirmation as proof of purchase.

Question:
Why does the listing say 0.6 L is recommended?
Answer:
The kettle holds 0.8 L, but above 0.6 L the water is higher than the base of the spout, so it can dribble when you start to pour. If you are only heating water to fill a teapot quickly, 0.8 L is fine. For slow pour-over, fill to 0.6 L.
