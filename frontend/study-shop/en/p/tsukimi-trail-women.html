Product description

Moonview Trail is a small outdoor brand with a workshop in Moonview Spa, at the end of the Vale Rail Mirror Line. Its founders guide on Mount Moonview and kept hearing the same complaint from women in their groups: unisex rain jackets are wide in the shoulders and stop at the waist, so the top of your trousers is wet by lunchtime. This jacket is their answer.

Between a city jacket and a mountain shell: 15,000 mm keeps out steady rain for a full day hike and holds up to the straps of a light day pack. For all-day storms with a heavy pack, a 20,000 mm 3-layer shell is the safer choice. Breathability is 10,000 g/m²/24 h, but there are no pit zips, and a backpack covers the back vent, so on a steep, warm climb open the front zip from the top and slow your pace to avoid getting damp inside.

Fit: sizes follow Japanese women's sizing (XS = 5, S = 7, M = 9, L = 11). The shoulders are narrower than a unisex jacket and the hips are fitted, so if you have fuller hips or will wear a fleece or thick trousers, choose the larger size. The sleeves are long; petite buyers under 150 cm find even the XS sleeves cover their hands, though the hook-and-loop cuffs can be tightened.

Care: wash it in a net at 30 °C or below with a little liquid detergent, no softener or bleach, rinse well and tumble dry on low, or iron on low through a cloth, to revive the water repellency. Use a spray-on re-proofer when water stops beading.

Teal is in stock at Marketa Springvale for next-day delivery; Plum ships from another Marketa warehouse and takes two to three days. Moonview Trail's 2-year warranty covers defects in seams, zips and fabric. Tears, snags and worn cuffs are repaired at their workshop at cost, from ¥1,500 plus postage, in about two weeks.
