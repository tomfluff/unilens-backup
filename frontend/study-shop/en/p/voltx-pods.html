Product description

VoltX Pods are basic wireless earbuds for podcasts, videos and the gym at a low price. Pair them once with your phone and they reconnect automatically whenever you open the case. The sound is clear in the middle range with light bass; there is no app and no equaliser.

There is no active noise cancelling. The ENC feature cleans up background noise in your voice during calls, but you still hear the train or the street. They connect to one device at a time: to switch from your phone to a tablet, disconnect the phone first. Like most budget earbuds they have a short audio delay, noticeable in games and in some video apps.

Battery: up to 5 hours at medium volume, less at high volume. The case holds about four more charges. If one earbud stops connecting, put both in the case, hold the touch areas on both earbuds for 10 seconds until the lights flash red and white, then pair again.

Sold and shipped by BrightDeal Trading from Kobe: delivery takes 4–8 days and is not eligible for Marketa Fast. BrightDeal gives a 6-month warranty; for any fault, contact the seller by Marketa message (weekdays 10:00–17:00).
