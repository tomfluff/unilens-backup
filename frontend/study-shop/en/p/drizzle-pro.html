Product description

A low-cost rain jacket to keep in the car, a bike basket or a desk drawer for sudden showers. The polyester shell has a PU coating on the inside that stops light and moderate rain, and the jacket folds into a drawstring bag.

About the 3,000 mm rating: this is enough for light rain and for moderate rain for a short time, such as a 10–20 minute walk or ride. In heavy rain, or where bag straps press on the fabric, water can be forced through, especially along seams. For long walks in heavy rain, or for hiking, choose a jacket rated 10,000 mm or higher.

Sizing: this is Asian sizing and the cut is small. Measure the chest of a jacket you own lying flat and compare it with the chart below; if you usually wear M in Japanese sizes, L or XL is likely to fit. Please check the size before opening the bag, because change-of-mind returns (including the wrong size) are accepted only unopened.

Care: a new PU coating has a smell when you open the bag; hang it in a well-ventilated place for a few days. Hand wash in cold water with a mild detergent and dry in the shade. Heat from a tumble dryer or iron, and dry-cleaning solvents, can make the coating peel or stick together. Do not store it folded while damp.

Ships from BrightDeal Trading's warehouse in Kobe with free shipping, separately from items shipped by Marketa, and is not eligible for Marketa Fast delivery. There is no manufacturer warranty; defects are handled by the seller within 30 days of delivery.
