Product description

The Seiran Studio NC are full-size headphones for long listening: flights, a whole day at a desk, the long train ride to Moonview Spa. The cups surround the ears rather than pressing on them, and at 265 g they are light for headphones of this size. Noise cancelling adapts automatically, or you can fix it at one level in the Seiran app.

Compatibility: any Bluetooth phone, tablet or computer. On Android phones that support LDAC you can turn on high-resolution playback in the app; iPhones use AAC, the best an iPhone offers over Bluetooth. Multipoint connects two devices at the same time, such as a phone and a laptop, but it switches off while LDAC is in use because LDAC needs the full Bluetooth bandwidth. Some older Windows laptops with Bluetooth 4.x have reported dropouts; firmware 2.1 improves this, so update in the app before you judge the connection.

Fit: the headband is fairly firm so that the cups seal for noise cancelling. People with larger heads or thick glasses frames may find it tight for the first week; it relaxes with use. The protein-leather pads are soft but warm in humid summer weather. Replacement pads cost ¥2,200 a pair from Seiran support and clip on without tools.

The included 3.5 mm cable works even when the battery is flat, without noise cancelling; with the headphones switched on, noise cancelling also works while wired. Seiran covers the headphones for two years from purchase, including the hinges, and the battery if it falls below 70% of its original capacity.
