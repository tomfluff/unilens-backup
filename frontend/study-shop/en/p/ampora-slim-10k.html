Product description

The Ampora Slim 10K is a light everyday power bank for your phone. It is as thin as a phone in a case and weighs 180 g, so it slips into a jacket pocket or a small bag for a long day at university or an evening at the Lantern Festival.

About capacity: as with every power bank, the 10,000 mAh figure is for the internal cells at 3.7 V. Your phone charges at 5 V or more, and some energy is lost as heat, so about 6,000 mAh reaches the phone. That is roughly two full charges of a phone with a 3,000–4,000 mAh battery, or 1.3 charges of a phone with a 5,000 mAh battery.

Compatibility: the USB-C port supports USB Power Delivery up to 20 W, which fast-charges recent iPhones and Android phones. The included cable is USB-C at both ends; for an older iPhone, use your own cable in the USB-A or USB-C port. It cannot charge laptops: a laptop that needs 30 W or more will not start charging.

To recharge it, use a USB-C charger of 18 W or more: about 3.5 hours. An old 5 V phone charger takes about 6 hours. It can charge your phone while it is being charged itself (pass-through), more slowly. Ampora covers it for 18 months from purchase; if the battery ever swells, stop using it and contact Ampora support for a free replacement.
