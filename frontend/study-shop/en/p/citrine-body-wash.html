Product description

Citrine Lab's Yuzu & Hinoki Body Wash is an everyday body wash for people whose skin feels tight or itchy after ordinary soap, especially in the dry winter months. Instead of sulfate cleansers (SLS, SLES), it uses mild amino-acid surfactants and is weakly acidic, close to the skin's own pH.

About the scent: the fresh citrus top note and soft cypress-wood base come from fragrance (香料). Yuzu fruit extract and hinoki water are included in small amounts for skin conditioning and do not give the scent. If you want a natural yuzu scent, look at products scented with yuzu peel oil instead; this one smells more like a hotel shower than a yuzu orchard.

How to use: amino-acid washes lather less than sulfate soaps. Two pumps on a wet body towel or foaming net make a soft, fine lather; on bare hands you will need more. Rinse well. Polyquaternium-10 leaves skin feeling lightly conditioned rather than squeaky clean.

Sizes: the 480 mL pump bottle lasts one person about two months at two pumps a day. The 400 mL refill pouch has a tear-off corner and fills the bottle to about four-fifths; rinse and dry the bottle before refilling. The pump with two refills (1,280 mL) costs ¥3,180, ¥260 less than ¥3,440 for the three bought separately. See the table below.

Free from sulfates, silicones, parabens and colouring; contains fragrance and the preservatives sodium benzoate and phenoxyethanol. Suitable for adults and children from 3 years. Stop using it if your skin becomes red or itchy. Unopened items can be returned within 30 days; opened toiletries only if defective or damaged.
