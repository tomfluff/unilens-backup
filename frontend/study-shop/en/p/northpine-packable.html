Product description

The Northpine Packable is a light, everyday rain jacket for town: the walk to the station, a bike ride to campus, or an evening at the Lantern Festival when the forecast is uncertain. It folds into its own chest pocket, so it can live in a bag all year and come out only when the rain does.

What 10,000 mm means: in a lab test the fabric holds back the pressure of a 10-metre column of water before it leaks. It is not an amount of rain. In practice it keeps you dry in heavy rain for an hour or two of walking. Water can still get through where the pressure is higher, for example under backpack straps over several hours, or where wind drives rain into the hood opening. The 8,000 g/m²/24 h breathability lets sweat escape at a walking pace, but on a steep climb such as the trail up Mount Tsukimi you sweat faster than it can escape and the inside feels clammy.

Fit: Northpine cuts the jacket close to the body to save weight. Most people who wear it over a T-shirt or shirt take their usual size; if you will wear a fleece, a thick sweater or a suit jacket underneath, take one size up. The sleeves are on the short side for people with long arms. Measure a jacket you like lying flat and compare it with the chart below.

Care: the outer fabric has a water-repellent finish that makes rain bead up and roll off. Dirt, sweat and skin oils wear it down; when rain starts to soak into the outer fabric (it looks darker in patches), the jacket feels cold even though it is not leaking. Wash it in a net on a gentle cycle at 30 °C or below with a little liquid detergent, no fabric softener or bleach, and rinse twice. Then tumble dry on low for 20 minutes, or iron on low through a cloth, to reactivate the finish. If water still does not bead, use a spray-on re-proofer for outdoor clothing. Do not dry-clean.

Navy XS–XL is in stock at Marketa Harukawa (HRK1) for next-day delivery. Navy XXL (¥500 more, with longer sleeves and back) and every Yellow size ship from another Marketa warehouse and take two to three days. Olive is sold out with no restock date. The 1-year Northpine warranty covers defects; keep your Marketa order confirmation as proof of purchase.

What does 10,000 mm actually mean? Is it enough for heavy rain?
Answer:
It is a lab test: the fabric holds back the pressure of a 10-metre column of water before it leaks. It is not an amount of rain, so the earlier answer here is not correct.
