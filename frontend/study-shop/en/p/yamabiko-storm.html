Product description

The Yamabiko Storm is a full mountain shell: the jacket you want when rain is forecast all day above the tree line, the wind is up and you are carrying a pack. If you mostly need a jacket for town, the train and the occasional shower, it is more than you need; the 210 g Northpine Packable costs about a third as much.

The 3-layer construction bonds the membrane between the outer fabric and a thin inner backer, so nothing rubs the membrane and there is no loose lining to cling when you sweat. At 20,000 mm it holds up to the pressure of pack straps and kneeling on wet ground, and at 25,000 g/m²/24 h it is among the most breathable shells at this price. On a climb such as the three-hour trail from Tsukimi Onsen to the summit of Mount Tsukimi, open the pit zips before you start to sweat, not after.

Fit: the cut leaves room for a fleece and a light down jacket, so most buyers who wear it over a base layer only find it slightly large and take one size down. If you are between sizes and hike in cold weather, keep your usual size. The back hem is 4 cm longer than the front to cover your lower back when you lean forward, and the sleeves still reach the base of your thumbs with your arms raised.

Care: wash it whenever it gets dirty; sweat and sunscreen clog the membrane and wear down the water-repellent finish. Use a little liquid detergent or a technical wash at 30 °C, no softener or bleach, and tumble dry on low for 20–30 minutes, which revives the finish. When water no longer beads after drying, use a wash-in or spray-on re-proofer.

Red and Black are both in stock at Marketa Harukawa in S–L, and Red also in XL; Black XL ships from another Marketa warehouse. Yamabiko's 2-year warranty covers seam tape that lifts, delamination and faulty zips; keep your Marketa order confirmation. Tears and worn cuffs are repaired at cost.

Question:
Will the hood fit over a climbing helmet?
Answer:
Yes. The hood fits over most climbing and cycling helmets up to about 62 cm head circumference. Loosen the rear adjuster first. Without a helmet, tighten the rear and both face adjusters so the hood turns with your head.

Question:
How often should I wash it, and how do I re-proof it?
Answer:
Wash it whenever it is dirty, and after every few hikes in summer. Use a little liquid detergent at 30 °C with no softener, then tumble dry on low for 20–30 minutes; the heat reactivates the water-repellent finish. When water no longer beads after drying, apply a wash-in re-proofer, or a spray-on one to the shoulders and forearms, and tumble dry again.
By Yamabiko Customer Support on 27 March 2026
