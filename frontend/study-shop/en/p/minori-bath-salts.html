Product description

The Hirose family has grown yuzu on the hillsides of the Mirror River valley, near Moonview Spa, since 1978. After the juice is pressed for our ponzu and marmalade, the peel is cold-pressed for its oil, and what is left is dried and milled into a fine powder. We mix both with sea salt from Harvest Bay at the farm. That is the whole recipe.

How to use: add one level scoop (30 g, scoop included) to a 180–200 L bath and stir. The salt dissolves within a minute; the peel powder floats, then settles, so you may see a few flecks and a little grit at the bottom of the tub. The scent is strongest as the salt dissolves and softens after 15–20 minutes, because real citrus oil evaporates. Rinse the tub and any chrome fittings after draining so the salt does not leave marks.

Reheating and bath equipment: you can reheat (追い焚き) with one-hole and two-hole systems, but the fine peel powder collects in the filter on the circulation port; pull it out and rinse it after the last bath of the day. Do not use it in 24-hour circulating baths, jetted baths, or wooden or natural-stone tubs, and we do not recommend using the leftover water for laundry. See the tables below.

Skin: yuzu peel oil contains natural citrus compounds such as limonene, which can sting broken skin and cause redness in sensitive people. Patch test on your inner arm before the first bath, and do not use it on eczema, cuts, sunburn or freshly shaved skin. Children from 3 years can use half a scoop. Stop if your skin becomes red or itchy.

Storage and returns: close the zip tightly and keep the pouch out of the bathroom. In humid weather the salt can set into a block, which you can break up with a spoon handle; the scent is not affected. Use within 6 months of opening for the best scent. The pouches are stored and shipped by Marketa, so they arrive the next day and follow Marketa's return policy: unopened within 30 days; opened only if defective or damaged.
