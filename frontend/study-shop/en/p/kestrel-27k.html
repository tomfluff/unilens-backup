Product description

The Kestrel 27K is built for large laptops. Its USB-C1 port supports USB Power Delivery 3.1 at up to 140 W, the same as the chargers that come with 16-inch performance laptops, so they charge at full speed instead of slowly losing battery as they do on a 65 W power bank such as the Ampora Go 20K.

To get 140 W you need a cable rated for 240 W (USB PD 3.1, often marked "EPR"). The included 1 m cable is; most ordinary USB-C cables top out at 60 W or 100 W, and the display will show the lower figure. 13–14-inch laptops, tablets and phones charge from any port at their own maximum speed.

Flying: 99.9 Wh is printed on the case, just under the 100 Wh line, so you can carry it on without airline approval. Do not cover the label with stickers, because security staff check it. Power banks are never allowed in checked baggage. Between 100 and 160 Wh you would need the airline's approval and could carry at most two.

At 140 W the aluminium shell gets warm, up to about 45 °C; this is normal and helps it shed heat. Use it on a table rather than inside a bag. For safety it ships at about 30–40% charge, so charge it fully before a trip. Kestrel covers it for two years from purchase.
