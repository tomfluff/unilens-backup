Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions we hear most, with links to the full rules. Times and dates assume an address in Harukawa city and an order placed today, Monday 5 October.

Orders and delivery

It is 10:40. Can my order still arrive today?
Yes, if the address is in Harukawa city and the item is in stock at HRK1: order by 11:00 for same-day delivery between 18:00 and 22:00. It costs ¥900; Fast members pay nothing on orders of ¥2,000 or more and ¥300 below that. Same-day delivery.
What is the latest time to order for next-day delivery?
14:00 for Harukawa city and the rest of Minori Prefecture, 12:00 for neighbouring prefectures and 10:00 for the rest of Honshu, Shikoku and Kyushu. Next-day delivery is not available to Hokkaido, Okinawa or remote islands. It costs ¥600 (¥800 to the rest of Honshu etc.) and is free for Fast members on Fast-eligible items.
Why does the Matte Black kettle arrive later than the Silver one?
Colours and sizes can be stocked in different warehouses. The Silver Corvo Kettle is at HRK1 in Harukawa (next day, Tue 6 Oct); the Matte Black is at a Marketa warehouse outside Minori Prefecture, so it arrives Thu 8 Oct for Fast members and Sat 10 Oct by standard delivery.
I ordered from Marketa and Kagami Kiln together. Why are there two parcels and two delivery fees?
Kagami Kiln packs and ships its pottery itself from Kamagaoka, so it arrives separately (Thu 8 – Fri 9 Oct) with the kiln’s own fee: ¥700, free on Kagami Kiln orders of ¥5,000 or more. Marketa Fast does not cover sellers’ shipping. Sellers that ship themselves.
Will the Lantern Festival affect my delivery?
Only inside the closure area in central Harukawa (around the castle, Ōtemachi, Honmachi, Kawabata and the riverside road). On Sat 10 Oct (closed 14:00–22:30) and Sun 11 Oct (closed 12:00–21:30), morning deliveries go ahead; afternoon and evening deliveries move to the next morning, and same-day delivery is not available there. Details.
Can I collect my parcel at Harukawa Central Station?
Yes. Choose the Marketa Locker at Harukawa Central Station (by the Kagami Line ticket gates, 5:00–24:30). Standard delivery to a locker is always free. Parcels are held for 3 days and must fit 45 × 35 × 30 cm and 10 kg; no cool delivery or cash on delivery.
I missed my delivery. What now?
Request a free redelivery on Track your package or from the email link; ask by 17:00 to get it the same evening. The depot keeps the parcel for 7 days (cool parcels 3 days).

Returns and refunds

Can I return earbuds I have tried?
Not if they are in-ear earbuds and you broke the hygiene seal, unless they are faulty. With the seal intact, you can return them within 30 days. Over-ear and open-ear headphones can be returned opened, with a 15% restocking fee.
How much do I get back for opened headphones I don’t like?
Item price minus 15% minus ¥550 return shipping. For Seiran Studio NC (¥24,800): ¥24,800 − ¥3,720 − ¥550 = ¥20,530. A paid Fast member does not pay the ¥550: ¥21,080.
My kettle arrived dented. What do I do?
Report it within 7 days of delivery in Your Orders with photos of the kettle, the box and the label. You get a free pick-up and a full refund including delivery fees, or a replacement sent next day if it is in stock at HRK1.
I am a Fast member. Are all my returns free?
Change-of-mind returns are free only for paid members (not during the free trial) and only for items sold by Marketa. Items sold by other sellers, even if shipped from Marketa, cost ¥550 to return, and the 15% restocking fee on opened electronics still applies.
How long does a refund take?
Inspection takes 1–2 business days after the return reaches us. Then: card 3–5 business days; Marketa Points immediately (+5% bonus); convenience-store payment or cash on delivery by bank transfer within 5–7 business days; Kumo Mobile carrier billing on the next or following bill.
Can I return a Kagami Kiln mug because the glaze looks different from the photo?
Kagami Kiln does not treat variation in glaze, colour or size, or small pinholes, as a defect: it is the nature of handmade ware. You can still return an unused item within 14 days of delivery and pay the return shipping yourself. Engraved and made-to-order items cannot be returned. Kagami Kiln’s policy.
BrightDeal Trading has not answered my return request.
BrightDeal replies on weekdays 10:00–17:00, by message only. If there is no reply within 2 business days, use Marketa Purchase Protection: Your Orders > "Problem with this order" > "Ask Marketa to step in". Claims are possible up to 90 days after the latest estimated delivery date. Purchase Protection.
My power bank stopped working after five months.
After 30 days, the warranty applies. Ampora power banks have an 18-month manufacturer’s warranty and the Kestrel 27K 2 years; contact the manufacturer with your order confirmation, or chat with us. The TitanCell 40000 from BrightDeal has a 6-month seller warranty, so contact BrightDeal now, before it runs out.

Marketa Fast

How do I get the free trial?
Choose "Try Fast free for 30 days" at checkout or on the Fast page. You need a credit card or Kumo Mobile carrier billing. Unless you cancel before the 30 days end, it becomes the ¥600 monthly plan.
I had a trial last year. Can I have another one?
Only if your last trial or membership ended more than 12 months ago. The trial is once per account; otherwise you can join the paid plan straight away.
Is there a student discount?
Yes: Fast Student is ¥300 a month or ¥2,950 a year, with a 6-month free trial if you have a university email address (for example at Harukawa University). The benefits are the same as Fast.
If I cancel my annual membership, do I get money back?
Within 14 days of paying, and only if you have not used any benefit, you get the full ¥5,900 back. Otherwise you get one-twelfth of the fee for each unused full month: with 7 months left, ¥3,441. Monthly plans are not refunded; they run to the end of the paid month.
Does Fast make shipping free for Kagami Kiln or Minori Yuzu Farm?
No. Items that sellers ship themselves use the seller’s fees: Kagami Kiln ¥700 (free on ¥5,000+), Minori Yuzu Farm ¥600 (free on ¥4,000+). The farm’s bath salts, hand cream and marmalade set ship from Marketa, so those are free for members and arrive next day.

Payment and points

Can I pay at a convenience store?
Yes, up to ¥299,999 and with no fee. Pay within 3 days (by 23:59 on the third day) or the order is cancelled. We ship after the payment is confirmed, so next-day delivery counts from your payment, not your order.
Why hasn’t my card been charged yet?
Cards are charged when the item is dispatched. At ordering we only place an authorisation hold. Made-to-order items, such as a Kagami Kiln amber donabe, are charged when they leave the kiln 3–4 weeks later.
Can I pay for a ¥5,980 item in instalments?
No. Instalments (3, 6, 10, 12 or 24) are available on orders of ¥10,000 or more, by credit card. Instalment fees are set by your card issuer.
When do my Marketa Points expire?
12 months after the last time you earned points. Each time you earn points, all your points are extended. You can see the date in Your Account > Marketa Points.
Can I buy a gift card with Kumo Mobile carrier billing?
No. Carrier billing cannot be used for Marketa Gift Cards. For everything else the limit is ¥50,000 a month (¥10,000 if you are under 20).
Can I use cash on delivery with a locker?
No. Cash on delivery (¥330, up to ¥300,000) is for home delivery only, and not for cool delivery or items that sellers ship themselves. Pay by card, points or at a convenience store instead.

Deals, coupons and sellers

When does the Lantern Festival Sale end?
Sun 11 Oct at 23:59. It started on Fri 2 Oct. Lightning Deals change every day, and Fast members can buy each one 30 minutes early. Today’s deals.
How do coupons work?
Clip the coupon on the product page; it is applied automatically at checkout. Each coupon can be used once per customer. Coupons marked "Fast members" work only with an active membership, including the free trial.
What is the difference between "ships from Marketa" and "ships from the seller"?
"Sold by seller, ships from Marketa" means the item is stored at our warehouse: Marketa’s delivery speeds, fees and 30-day return policy apply, and it is Fast-eligible. "Ships from the seller" means the seller packs and sends it: their own fees, times and return policy apply.
Is the Tokiya mug pair real Harukawa ware?
No. The Tokiya "Harukawa-style" mug pair sold by BrightDeal Trading is factory-made in China in a Harukawa style. Genuine Harukawa ware on Marketa comes from Kagami Kiln in Kamagaoka, Harukawa.
