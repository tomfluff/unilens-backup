# Kumo Mobile Support: candidate study tasks

Today on the site is Sunday 4 October 2026. Prices include tax. Paths are under `/en/` and `/ja/` (same path in both languages).

## 1. Cheapest plan for a family of three

- **EN:** "We are a family of three and each of us uses about 15 GB a month. Which plan is cheapest, and what would we pay?"
- **JA:** 「3人家族で、それぞれ月15GBくらい使います。いちばん安いプランはどれで、月いくらになりますか？」
- **Where:** `plans/index.html` (comparison table, family discount table, price chart), `plans/basic.html` (price-with-discounts table).
- **Why interesting:** needs comparing four plans, reading the family discount by number of lines and ruling out Mini with extra data. The chart helps but uses no discounts.
- **Answer:** Kumo Basic. ¥4,378 − ¥1,100 (3 lines) = ¥3,278 per line, **¥9,834 a month** for three lines (plus ¥4 per line in universal/relay fees). With Kumo Hikari at home: ¥2,178 per line (¥6,534). Kumo Unlimited would be ¥6,138 per line; Kumo Mini with 12 GB extra ¥8,778 per line.

## 2. Switching when the contract is in a parent's name

- **EN:** "My phone contract is in my mother's name. I want to switch to Kumo in my own name. What do I need to bring?"
- **JA:** 「今の携帯は母の名義です。自分の名義でクモモバイルに乗り換えたいのですが、何を持っていけばいいですか？」
- **Where:** `procedures/switch-to-kumo.html#other-name`, `forms/checklist.html`, `forms/index.html`, `procedures/transfer.html`.
- **Why interesting:** three alternative routes; conditions spread across a procedure page, the checklist and the forms list.
- **Answer:** Either change the holder at the current carrier first, or switch in the mother's name and transfer at Kumo (¥3,850), or switch with a change of holder at a shop (family only, not online). For the last: your ID; your mother's ID (original, or a copy plus form K-12 signed by her); form K-12; a family register or certificate of residence issued within 3 months if surname or address differ; the MNP reservation number obtained by your mother (One-Stop cannot be used); your payment card. Admin fee ¥3,850.

## 3. Cost of cancelling mid-month

- **EN:** "I'm on Kumo Basic with Talk 5 and Device Care Standard. If I cancel on 12 October, what will I pay for October?"
- **JA:** 「クモ ベーシックにクモ通話5分とクモ端末補償スタンダードを付けています。10月12日に解約すると、10月分はいくらになりますか？」
- **Where:** `procedures/cancel.html` (fees table and worked example), `faq.html#changes`.
- **Why interesting:** the asymmetry (first month prorated, last month not) and the knock-on effect on family members are in different sections.
- **Answer:** No cancellation fee, but the full month: ¥4,378 + ¥880 + ¥880 = **¥6,138** plus calls until the 12th (discounts still apply to October). Remaining device instalments continue (or are paid at once). Other family lines lose part of their discount from November. Exception: cancellation after the holder's death is charged by the day.

## 4. MNP reservation number about to expire

- **EN:** "My MNP reservation number expires in 6 days. Can I still apply online?"
- **JA:** 「MNP予約番号の有効期限があと6日です。オンラインで申し込めますか？」
- **Where:** `procedures/switch-to-kumo.html` ("Two ways to switch" box, small print), `faq.html#joining`, `forms/checklist.html`.
- **Why interesting:** the answer is in grey small print.
- **Answer:** Not online (at least 10 days must remain), but yes at a shop (at least 5 days must remain). Or get a new number.

## 5. A plan for a grandparent

- **EN:** "My father is 68. Can he get Kumo 65+ if I pay the bill? We have three lines and Kumo Hikari. How much would his line cost?"
- **JA:** 「68歳の父に、私の支払いでクモ 65+を使わせられますか？家族で3回線、クモ光も契約しています。父の回線はいくらになりますか？」
- **Where:** `plans/senior.html`, `plans/index.html#family`, `plans/index.html#home-bundle`.
- **Why interesting:** "registered user" vs "contract holder"; Kumo 65+ gets a flat ¥550 family discount whatever the number of lines.
- **Answer:** Yes: the registered user must be 65+, the contract holder can be the child. ¥2,728 − ¥550 (family, flat) − ¥550 (home bundle) = **¥1,628 a month**. Watch Service included and no paper bill fee.

## 6. Lost phone during maintenance

- **EN:** "I lost my phone and it's 3 a.m. on 8 October. How do I stop my line?"
- **JA:** 「10月8日の午前3時にスマホをなくしました。回線を止めるにはどうすればいいですか？」
- **Where:** `procedures/lost-phone.html`, `status.html#maintenance`, `news/maintenance-october-2026.html`, `contact.html`.
- **Why interesting:** the normal online route is unavailable at that time; the warning is in a maintenance table and a note.
- **Answer:** My Kumo is down 01:00–06:00 on 8 October, so call the Lost and Stolen line 0120-58-0110 (24 hours; 1581 from another Kumo phone). Suspension is free; the plan fee continues. If suspended by phone, the line cannot be resumed online later (call again or visit a shop with ID).

## 8. When does Kumo Unlimited become worth it?

- **EN:** "At how many GB a month is Kumo Unlimited cheaper than Kumo Basic?"
- **JA:** 「月何GB以上使うと、クモ ベーシックよりクモ アンリミテッドのほうが安くなりますか？」
- **Where:** price chart on `plans/index.html#chart` (an SVG chart), `plans/options.html#data`, `news/price-revision-2026.html`.
- **Why interesting:** a chart reading, then a correction from fine print (5 GB pack) and the December price change.
- **Answer:** About 25 GB with extra data bought 1 GB at a time (Basic + 6 GB = ¥7,678 at 26 GB vs ¥7,238). Buying the 5 GB pack (¥2,200) moves it to about 27 GB (Basic + 5 GB pack + 1 GB = ¥7,128 at 26 GB). From 1 December Unlimited is ¥7,458.

## 9. Under-22 discount end

- **EN:** "My son turns 22 in February 2027. He's on Kumo Unlimited with the under-22 discount. Until when does he get it, and how much is it?"
- **JA:** 「息子が2027年2月に22歳になります。クモ アンリミテッドでU22割を受けていますが、いつまで、いくら割引されますか？」
- **Where:** `plans/index.html#u22`, `faq.html#discounts`.
- **Answer:** ¥1,100 a month on Unlimited (¥550 on Basic), up to and including the bill for February 2027 (the month he turns 22). It is based on the registered user's age, even if the contract is in a parent's name.

## 10. Roaming in December

- **Where:** `plans/roaming.html` (zone table), `news/price-revision-2026.html`.

## 11. ID with only a passport

- **EN:** "I only have a passport issued in 2022 and my old health insurance card. Can I sign up online?"
- **JA:** 「2022年に発行されたパスポートと、以前の健康保険証しかありません。オンラインで契約できますか？」
- **Where:** `procedures/new-contract.html#id`, `news/index.html` (notice of 10 June), `faq.html#joining`.
- **Answer:** No. Online needs a My Number card or driver's licence. At a shop, the passport needs a supplementary document (utility bill or certificate of residence issued within 3 months), because passports since February 2020 have no address field. Old-style health insurance cards are not accepted since 1 July 2026.

## 15. Transferring a contract to a daughter

- **EN:** "My daughter has started work and wants her line in her own name. How much does it cost, who pays, and does she keep her number?"
- **JA:** 「娘が就職したので、回線を娘の名義にしたいです。費用はいくらで、誰が払いますか？電話番号は変わりませんか？」
- **Where:** `procedures/transfer.html`, `billing/fees.html`, `forms/checklist.html`.
- **Answer:** Transfer at a shop only, ¥3,850 paid by the new holder (the daughter). Both IDs and form K-15 (or K-07 plus the parent's original ID if the parent cannot come), relationship document if surname/address differ. Number, plan and email address stay. Remaining device instalments pass to her after a credit check, or the parent pays them off first. The month's plan fee is charged to her in full.
