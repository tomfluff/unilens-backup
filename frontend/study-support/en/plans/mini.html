Kumo Mini (3 GB)

Our lowest-priced plan, for people who mostly use Wi-Fi at home and need a little mobile data when out.

Monthly price
¥2,178
Data
3 GB / month
Calls
¥22 / 30 s

What happens after 3 GB

When you have used 3 GB in a month, your speed is limited to 300 kbps until the 1st of the next month. That is enough for text messages, but web pages and maps load slowly and video does not play smoothly. You can buy extra data at any time in My Kumo:

Extra dataPriceValid for
1 GB¥55062 days from purchase
5 GB¥2,20062 days from purchase

Extra data is used after your monthly 3 GB. We send an SMS when you reach 80% and 100% of your allowance.

Using more data?

If you regularly use more than 5 GB, Kumo Basic (¥4,378) is likely to be cheaper. You can change plan once a month; the change applies from the 1st of the next month, or from the same day when you move up to a larger plan.

Discounts

  • Family discount: a Kumo Mini line is not discounted, but it counts towards the number of lines in your family group, so it can raise the discount for other members. Details
  • Home internet bundle: ¥550 off per month with Kumo Hikari. Details
  • Under-22 discount: not available.

Fine print

  • Unused data does not carry over.
  • Tethering is allowed and counts towards your 3 GB.
  • Kumo World roaming passes are available. See International roaming.
  • Prices include consumption tax. The universal service fee (¥3/month) and telephone relay service fee (¥1/month) are charged separately.
  • In the month you sign up, the plan fee is charged by the day. In the month you cancel, it is charged in full.
  • Plan changes take effect from the 1st of the next month. See Change your plan.
  • Calls to numbers starting with 0570 (Navi Dial), 188 and other special numbers are charged and are not included in free call allowances.
