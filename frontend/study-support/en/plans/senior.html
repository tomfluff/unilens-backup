Kumo 65+ (for users aged 65 and over)

A simple plan with 5 GB of data, free calls up to 10 minutes and the Watch Service included.

Monthly price
¥2,728
Data
5 GB / month
Calls
Free up to 10 min

Who can apply

The registered user must be aged 65 or over on the day of application. The contract holder can be someone else, for example a son or daughter who pays the bill. Bring an ID document showing the user's date of birth. When you change to Kumo 65+ from another plan, the user's age is checked again.

What is included

  • Free domestic calls up to 10 minutes each. After 10 minutes, a call is charged at ¥22 per 30 seconds. Upgrade to unlimited calls for ¥1,100 a month.
  • 5 GB of data. After 5 GB, speed is limited to 300 kbps. Extra data: 1 GB ¥550.
  • Watch Service (normally ¥330/month): sends a daily message to up to 3 family members when the phone has not been used for 24 hours. Included free since 1 September 2026.
  • No paper bill fee. The ¥209 paper bill fee is waived.
  • Free smartphone classes at many shops (booking required). See Kumo Shop Harukawa Central.

Discounts

  • Family discount: ¥550 a month whenever the family group has 2 or more lines (the amount does not increase with more lines).
  • Home internet bundle: ¥550 a month.
  • Under-22 discount: not applicable.

Example: a Kumo 65+ line in a group of 3 lines with Kumo Hikari costs ¥2,728 − ¥550 − ¥550 = ¥1,628 a month.

Fine print

  • Free call minutes do not apply to calls to 0570 numbers, international calls or calls made while abroad.
  • Each call is timed separately; a call that lasts 12 minutes is charged for 2 minutes (¥88).
  • If the registered user is changed to someone under 65, the plan is changed to Kumo Basic from the following month.
  • Prices include consumption tax. The universal service fee (¥3/month) and telephone relay service fee (¥1/month) are charged separately.
  • In the month you sign up, the plan fee is charged by the day. In the month you cancel, it is charged in full.
  • Plan changes take effect from the 1st of the next month. See Change your plan.
  • Calls to numbers starting with 0570 (Navi Dial), 188 and other special numbers are charged and are not included in free call allowances.
