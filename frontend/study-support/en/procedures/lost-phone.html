Lost or stolen phone

Suspend your line straight away to stop anyone else using it. Suspension is free and available 24 hours a day.

Suspend your line now

Lost and Stolen line (24 hours, free): 0120-58-0110
From another Kumo phone: 1581 · From abroad: +81-3-6300-0110 (charged)

Steps

  1. Suspend your line

    In My Kumo on another device (a computer or a family member's phone): Support → Lost or stolen → Suspend. Or call the Lost and Stolen line. Suspension stops calls, SMS and data immediately.

  2. Lock or locate your phone

    Use Find My (iPhone) or Find My Device (Android) from another device. You can show a message with a contact number on the lock screen.

  3. Report to the police

    Report the loss or theft at a police box (kōban) or police station. Note the receipt number: you need it for a Device Care claim.

  4. Replace your phone or SIM

    With Device Care Standard or Premium, claim a replacement (¥12,100 / ¥8,800). Otherwise buy a new phone. Get a new SIM: ¥2,200 in My Kumo or ¥3,850 at a shop; eSIM ¥0 in My Kumo.

  5. Resume your line

    When you have your phone or a new SIM, resume the line in My Kumo or at a shop.

Fees while suspended

  • Suspension and resumption: free.
  • Your plan fee and options continue to be charged while the line is suspended. No call or data charges are made.
  • If you will not use the number for a long time, consider Number Keep (¥440/month).

If you find your phone

If you suspended in My Kumo, you can resume in My Kumo. If you suspended by phone, you cannot resume online: call the Lost and Stolen line again from the phone's number, or visit a shop with your ID. If you reported the loss to the police, tell them it has been found.

Planned maintenance

My Kumo is unavailable on 8 October 2026 from 01:00 to 06:00 for maintenance. During this time, suspend your line by phone. Details
