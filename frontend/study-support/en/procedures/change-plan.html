Change your plan

Plan changes are free and take effect from the 1st of the next month. When you move to a larger plan, you can start it today.

When the new plan starts

ChangeStartsMonthly fee this monthDeadline
Any plan → a smaller plan1st of next monthCurrent planLast day of the month, 21:00 (My Kumo) or shop closing time
Any plan → a larger plan (normal)1st of next monthCurrent planLast day of the month, 21:00
Any plan → a larger plan ("start today")TodayNew plan in full; the current plan is not charged25th of the month

"Larger" means Kumo Mini → Kumo Basic, Kumo Mini → Kumo Unlimited, Kumo Basic → Kumo Unlimited, or Kumo 65+ → Kumo Unlimited.

Example: changing on 18 October

  • Kumo Mini → Kumo Basic, start today: October is charged ¥4,378 (Basic in full). You get 20 GB for October; data already used in October counts towards it.
  • Kumo Mini → Kumo Basic, from next month: October is charged ¥2,178; Basic starts on 1 November.
  • Kumo Unlimited → Kumo Basic: always from 1 November. October is charged as Kumo Unlimited.

Rules

  • One change per month. If you make several requests, the last one made before the deadline applies. You can cancel a pending change in My Kumo until 21:00 on the last day of the month.
  • Data carried over on Kumo Basic is lost when you change to another plan.
  • Changing to Kumo 65+ requires the registered user to be 65 or over; the user's age is checked (bring ID to a shop, or use My Number card online).
  • The under-22 discount ends if you change to Kumo Mini or Kumo 65+, and does not restart if you change back.
  • Changing to Kumo Mini removes the family discount from that line (it still counts as a line in the group).
  • Call options stay as they are, except that Talk Unlimited for Kumo 65+ ends when you leave Kumo 65+.

Steps in My Kumo

  1. Log in to My Kumo

    Use your Kumo ID and password, or the My Kumo app.

  2. Open "Contract" → "Change plan"

    Your current plan and any pending change are shown.

  3. Choose the new plan

    The monthly price with your current discounts is shown for each plan.

  4. Choose when it starts

    "From next month" or, for a larger plan before the 25th, "Start today".

  5. Confirm

    You receive an SMS and email. Fee: ¥0.

At a shop the change is also free, but please book (about 30 minutes).
