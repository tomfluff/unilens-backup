Repair and replacement

Broken screen, water damage or a phone that will not start: how to get it repaired or replaced, with or without Device Care.

Your options

Device Care memberNot a member
Delivery replacementA refurbished phone of the same model is delivered. Light: not available; Standard ¥3,300; Premium ¥0 for the first claimNot available
Repair through a shop¥5,500 (Light), ¥3,300 (Standard), ¥0 first claim then ¥3,300 (Premium)Estimate free. Screen repairs ¥19,800–¥42,900 depending on the model
Loaner phoneFree¥1,100 per repair
Repair time7–10 days7–14 days

Delivery replacement (Device Care Standard and Premium)

  1. Apply

    Apply in My Kumo or call Device Care (0120-58-0345, 9:00–20:00). Applications by 15:00 are delivered the next day (Moonview Town and Shiose Village: 2 days).

  2. Prepare the old phone

    Back up your data, turn off Find My / Find My Device, and remove the SIM and any memory card.

  3. Receive the replacement

    The courier hands you the replacement. Move your SIM to it, or reissue your eSIM in My Kumo (free).

  4. Return the old phone

    Put the old phone in the return pack and post it within 14 days. If it is not returned, a fee of ¥22,000 is charged.

Repair through a shop

These shops accept repairs: Kumo Shop Springvale Central, Kumo Shop Springvale Kitamachi, Kumo Shop Bayside Mall Ferry Port, Kumo Shop Mikage, Kumo Shop Moonview Spa. Kumo Shop Hanaoka and the Springvale University counter do not accept repairs. Book an appointment (about 30 minutes).

Before you bring the phone

  • Back up your data (the repair may erase it)
  • Turn off Find My (iPhone) or remove your Google account (Android)
  • Remove the case, screen protector, SIM and memory card
  • Bring your ID and, for Device Care, nothing else: we check your membership

Battery replacement

Device Care Premium: free once, 24 months or more after enrolment. Otherwise ¥9,900–¥14,300 depending on the model.

  • Water-damaged phones often cannot be repaired. With Device Care Standard or Premium you can choose a delivery replacement instead.
  • Phones repaired by an unauthorised repair shop may not be accepted.
  • Kumo cannot recover data from a broken phone.
