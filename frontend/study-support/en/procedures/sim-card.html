Insert a SIM card

Kumo SIM cards are nano-SIM size. It takes a minute; switch the phone off first.

Steps

  1. Switch off the phone

    Inserting or removing a SIM with the power on can damage the SIM or the data on it.

  2. Push the pin into the hole

    Insert the SIM ejector pin (1, 2) straight into the small hole next to the tray and press gently until the tray pops out.

  3. Place the SIM in the tray

    Line up the cut corner (3) with the cut corner of the tray. The gold contacts (4) face down, towards the phone.

  4. Push the tray back in

    Hold the phone flat and push the tray (5) in, the same way round as it came out, until it is flush.

  5. Switch on and check

    The status bar shows "Kumo" within a minute. Call 111 to test.

Take care

  • Do not use a needle, paper clip or earring: it can damage the mechanism. A pin is included with your phone, and shops give one free.
  • Do not cut a SIM card or use an adapter in the tray; cards can get stuck.
  • Do not touch the gold contacts.

No signal after inserting the SIM?

  1. Restart the phone.
  2. Check that airplane mode is off.
  3. Check the APN (Android): kumo.jp. See eSIM setup.
  4. Check Service status for problems in your area.
  5. If you switched from another carrier, check in My Kumo that you pressed "Switch my line".

Lost or damaged SIM: reissue ¥2,200 in My Kumo (delivered in 2–4 days) or ¥3,850 at a shop (same day).
