Book an appointment

Book a time at a Kumo shop online or by phone. Booking is free.

How long each procedure takes

ProcedureTime to bookNote
New contract or switching to Kumo90 minutesFor 3 or more lines, book two slots in a row
Device change (new phone)60 minutes
Contract transfer or inheritance60 minutesBoth people should come
eSIM setup or SIM reissue45 minutes
Plan change, options, address change30 minutesFree online in My Kumo
Repair or Device Care claim30 minutesNot at Hanaoka or the University counter
Smartphone class60 minutesSpringvale Central and Bayside Mall only

Steps

  1. Choose a shop

    See Find a shop. Check that the shop offers the service you need.

  2. Choose the procedure

    The booking time depends on the procedure (see the table above). One booking covers up to 2 lines of the same contract holder.

  3. Choose a date and time

    You can book from 30 days ahead until 1 hour before the start time. Times are shown in 15-minute steps.

  4. Enter your details

    Name, contact phone number and email. You do not need to be a Kumo customer.

  5. Receive your booking number

    An SMS with your booking number arrives within a few minutes. Show it at the reception.

Book online

Online booking is temporarily unavailable. Please call 0120-58-0789 (10:00–19:00).

Changes and cancellations

  • Change or cancel using the link in your booking SMS, up to 1 hour before the start time.
  • If you are more than 15 minutes late, your booking may be cancelled and you may have to wait as a walk-in.
  • If you miss two bookings within 3 months without cancelling, online booking is unavailable for 30 days.

Book by phone

Shop Booking Line: 0120-58-0789 (free), 10:00–19:00 every day.

Bring everything you need: see the What to bring checklist. Shop procedures have a ¥3,850 admin fee where the online procedure is free.
